Questions

How accurate is it?

With the sheet on the wall and all four wall corners in the photo, about one to two percent. With an iPhone Pro scan, a centimetre or two. From a photo with no sheet it estimates, says so, and lists what it assumed.

Do I need to be good with computers?

If you can make a folder and type a sentence, you're fine. Set-up is double-clicks and the videos show every step.

What's the catch with "no subscription"?

One thing it needs isn't ours: a Claude subscription, about $30 a month, paid to Anthropic. It's the brains. We don't take a cut, and you can cancel it whenever you like.

What if I get stuck?

Email us. A person replies. And if you decide you'd rather we ran it for you, your $249 becomes credit.

Is my stuff safe?

It all lives in one folder on your laptop. It never sends a quote or a message. You do.

Does it work with ServiceM8 or Xero?

It makes PDFs and a spreadsheet, and you can use those anywhere. It isn't plugged into them directly.
