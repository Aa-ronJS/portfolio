What it does

Photo apps guess. Nobody measures a room from one photo to better than about twenty percent, so this one doesn't try. It uses a sheet of paper as the ruler. Try the measuring page with any wall.

Measures

Stick any A4 sheet on the wall, printed or blank, and photograph the wall corner to corner. The app outlines the sheet and the wall, you confirm each, and the doors and windows it can see are listed. Or type the sizes. On Android there is an AR tape too, and an iPhone Pro LiDAR scan imports straight in.

Quotes

Rooms and a few notes in, a priced PDF out at your rates, with your licence, insurance and terms on it. Lines grouped by room. A private cost card shows your hours, paint and margin; the client never sees it. Send it from the app by email, or through your share sheet.

Invoices and gets paid

Deposit, progress and final invoices with your bank details, and a pay-by-card link when you connect Stripe. Record payments, print receipts and a statement. Tax invoice only when you are registered for GST.

Follows up

Quote follow-ups and invoice reminders on the days you set, on business days, in your words. Connect your own Twilio and Resend through a small relay and they send themselves, stopping the moment the client accepts or pays. Without that, the app shows you who to chase and you tap Text or Email.

Books

Client says yes, mark it accepted and the days go into your phone's calendar. Phone enquiry gives a ballpark while they are still on the line and picks visit slots with the least driving.

Backs up

One tap saves a back-up file you can put in your own Drive or iCloud. Restore it on a new phone. An optional PIN locks the app.

Put it on your home screen

It is a web app, so there is no app store. Open it once in your browser, then add it to your home screen and it behaves like any other app, offline included.

Use the same address every time. The app keeps its data per address, so a bookmark to a different copy starts empty.

iPhone

  1. Open the app in Safari, not inside another app.
  2. Tap Share, the square with the arrow at the bottom.
  3. Scroll down and tap Add to Home Screen, then Add.

Android

  1. Open the app in Chrome.
  2. Tap the three dots at the top right.
  3. Tap Install app (on some phones, Add to Home screen), then Install.

What it does not do (yet)

  • No cloud sync between phones. One phone holds the data; move it with the back-up file.
  • No accountant or bookkeeper login. Export the CSV and the PDFs and send those.
  • No online accept link for clients. They reply by text or email, or sign the acceptance block on the quote, and you mark it accepted.
  • No Square. Card payments are Stripe payment links only.
  • Not plugged into Xero, MYOB or ServiceM8.

It is not legal advice

The default terms, deposit rules and reminder wording are a starting point. Each state has its own rules on licence numbers, deposit caps and written contracts, and the app warns about the ones it knows. Check them for your state before your first real quote.

Questions

Is the app really free?

Yes. The phone app is free to use and there is no account. If you turn on automatic SMS and email, Twilio and Resend charge you their own usage fees directly, a few cents a message. If you take card payments, Stripe takes its usual cut. The $249 laptop pack is optional and separate.

How accurate is the measuring?

With any A4 sheet on the wall and the whole wall in the photo, about one and a half percent. No sheet handy? Tap the corners and scale from a door (2.04 m) or the ceiling height, which is a few percent. With an iPhone Pro scan, a centimetre or two. Typed sizes are what you typed, and the quote says the sizes were supplied by the client when they were.

Does it send messages by itself?

Only if you set that up. Connect your own Twilio and Resend accounts through the relay and quote follow-ups and invoice reminders go out on the days you set, on business days, and stop the moment the client accepts or pays. You can hold or cancel any of them from the Follow-ups tab, and there is a log of everything sent. Without the relay, the app shows you who to chase and you tap Text or Email.

Where is my data, and what if I lose my phone?

On the phone, in the app, and nowhere else unless you send something. That is the good news and the bad news. Save a back-up after every invoice and keep it in your own Drive or iCloud; restore it on the new phone. The privacy page lists what Twilio, Resend, Stripe and the relay see when you use them.

Does it work with Xero, MYOB or ServiceM8?

It makes PDFs and a back-up file, and exports your invoices and payments as CSV for your bookkeeper. It is not plugged into any of them directly.

What does the $249 pack add?

A way to run the same quoting from a laptop, typing /quote, /invoice and /chase into Claude Code, with your price list and wording in plain text files, plus seven short video lessons. It suits painters who do their admin at a desk and like to change things. It needs a Claude subscription, about $30 a month, paid to Anthropic, not to us. The phone app does not need it.

Do I need to be good with computers?

For the app, no. Set-up is your name, your bank details and a look at five prices. Turning on automatic sending means creating a Twilio and a Resend account and deploying the relay, which is a copy-and-paste job with instructions in the app; email us if you get stuck. The laptop pack expects you to be comfortable making a folder and typing a sentence.
