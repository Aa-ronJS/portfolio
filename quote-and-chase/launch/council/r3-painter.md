## THE 4-MESSAGE FACT

It changes the plan, not the tier. At the top of the band we agreed, 8 quotes a week, he is at roughly 136
messages on quotes alone and over 150 once the invoice chases go, so in week three he gets a top-up. What he

which is the gym membership and the tool-hire account all over again, and he cancels over $35 he was not
warned about, not over the money.

units before he pays and again on the offer screen — "150 messages is about 37 quotes chased to the end; a

busiest painter in the agreed band never buys a pack in a normal month.
