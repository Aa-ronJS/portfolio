# Meta ads: first campaign for Quote and Chase (painters)

## Structure

One campaign, sales objective, Purchase as the conversion event
(fallback: Lead until Purchase has data). Two ad sets, same creative,
different audiences, AUD 30 to 40 a day each, five days. Australia,
25 to 60. Advantage+ placements on; if the video exists, force Reels and
Stories to use the 9:16 cut.

- **Ad set A, the painter.** Interests: House painter, Painting and
  decorating, Dulux, Haymes Paint, Taubmans, Wattyl, Master Painters
  Australia, Tradie, ServiceM8, Tradify. Narrow with: Small business
  owners or Facebook page admins.
- **Ad set B, the person who does the admin.** Interests as above,
  narrowed with: Bookkeeping, Xero, Small business, plus "engaged
  shoppers". Women and men, 28 to 60. This is the partner or office
  manager who actually sits at the laptop at 9pm.

Kill rules after AUD 150 spent per ad set: link click-through under 1%
or cost per landing-page view over AUD 3, pause. Demo use rate under 10%
of visitors, the page is the problem, not the ad. Leads but no
purchases after 300 visitors, test AUD 199 before touching the copy.

**1. The evening one.**
You did not start a painting business to type quotes at 9pm.

Quote and Chase runs on your own laptop. Drop your phone photos into a
folder, type a few notes, and it prices the job from YOUR rates and
writes the PDF. When the job's done it writes the invoice, and on Monday
it lists who owes you and drafts the reminders.

You send everything yourself. It never quotes from anyone's prices but
yours. One-time price, no per-job fees, no SMS credits.

Point your phone at a wall and see it work: [link]

**2. The fee one.**
Per job. Per user. Per text message. Your job software charges more the
month you get busier.

Quote and Chase is a tool you own. Photos in, priced PDF quote out, in

$249 once. If you can't produce a quote from your own photos in 14
days, full refund.

Try it on any wall right now: [link]

**3. The short one.**
Take a photo of the room. Get a priced quote in two minutes. Your

For painters with one to five on the tools. Scan or tap to try it on a

## Headlines (pick two to test)

- Quote from a photo in two minutes
- Your prices. Your laptop. No per-job fees.
- Stop typing quotes at 9pm
- Photos in. PDF quote out.
- The office manager you don't have to pay weekly

## Descriptions

- One-time price. 14-day money-back guarantee.
- Built on Claude Code. Set up in three minutes.

## Creative

**Video (preferred):** the two-minute demo cut to 30 seconds. Open on the
phone taking a photo of a wall, cut to the laptop typing `/quote`, speed
through the run, land on the PDF total, end card with the headline and
URL. Captions on; most Reels play muted.

**Still (launch tonight if the video is not ready):** screenshot the
landing page hero's quote card on a plain background, headline text
"Quote from a photo in two minutes" top left. 1:1 and 4:5.

## Landing

Send every ad to the page root with UTM tags:
`?utm_source=meta&utm_medium=paid&utm_campaign=painters-launch&utm_content=A-evening`
The demo is the conversion mechanism on the page. The QR is for print
and for people watching the ad on a desktop.

## What not to say

No income or savings figures you cannot show. No "Anthropic approved"
or logos. No "replaces ServiceM8" in ad copy; say "no per-job fees"
instead. Do not target by age under 25; painters buying software are
older, and the CPMs are worse.
