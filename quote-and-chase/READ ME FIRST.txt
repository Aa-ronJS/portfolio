QUOTE AND CHASE FOR PAINTERS
============================

This add-on gives Claude Code three jobs for a painting business:
photos and notes in, PDF quote out; accepted quote in, PDF invoice out;
and a Monday list of who owes you, with the reminder messages drafted.

Before you start
  You need Claude Code set up first, using the setup files from the
  core course. That created a folder called "My Business" in your
  Documents. This add-on installs into that folder.

Install
  Windows: double-click "Install Quote and Chase (Windows).bat"
  Mac:     right-click "Install Quote and Chase (Mac).command", choose
           Open, then Open again.
  It is safe to run more than once. Your price list and business
  details are never overwritten once you have edited them.

Then
  Open "START HERE - Quote and Chase.txt" inside your My Business
  folder. It walks you through filling in your prices and running your
  first quote on the example job.

What it never does
  It never sends anything. You send every quote, invoice and reminder
  yourself. It never uses a price that is not in your price list.
