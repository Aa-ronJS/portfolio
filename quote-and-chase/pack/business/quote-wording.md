# Standard quote wording

Claude puts these blocks on every quote. Edit them once to match how
you work.

## What is included

- Protection of floors, furniture and fittings with drop sheets and
  plastic before work starts.
- Preparation as described in the quote: filling minor holes and
  cracks, light sanding, dusting and spot priming.
- Two full coats of quality trade paint in the colours you choose,
  applied with brush and roller (spray where noted).
- Daily clean up and removal of all our rubbish at the end of the job.
- All labour, materials, ladders and equipment.

## What is not included

- Moving heavy furniture, pianos or built-in items.
- Repairs to plaster, timber or render beyond the prep described.
- Testing or removal of lead paint or asbestos.
- Colour consulting. Please have colours chosen before we start.
- Anything not written in this quote. Extra work is agreed in writing
  before it starts and invoiced as a variation.

## Terms

- This quote is valid for 30 days from the date above.
- A 20% deposit confirms your booking. The balance is due within 7
  days of completion.
- Weather can move exterior dates. We will keep you informed.
- Workmanship is guaranteed for 5 years against peeling and flaking
  caused by our application.

## How to accept

Reply to this quote by text or email with "accepted" and your
preferred start week, and pay the deposit to the account on the last
page. We will confirm your date within one business day.

## Reminder tone

- First reminder (1 to 7 days overdue): friendly, assume they forgot.
- Second reminder (8 to 21 days): polite but clear, restate amount and
  due date, ask if there is a problem with the work.
- Final reminder (22+ days): firm, state the next step (payment plan or
  debt collection) and a date. Still courteous.
