IF SOMETHING GOES WRONG
=======================

The fastest fix for almost anything: copy the message on the screen,
paste it into Claude Code, and add "what does this mean and what should
I do?" It will usually tell you in plain English.

Common ones

  "claude is not recognised" or "command not found"
    Close the window. Run "1 - Setup Claude Code" from the setup folder
    again. Then use the Claude Code icon on your Desktop.

  It asks you to log in
    That is Anthropic's login for your Claude subscription. Type /login
    and press Enter; your browser opens. You need a Claude subscription
    for this to work.

  "/quote" says it cannot find the folder
    The folder must be inside quotes/incoming and the name you type
    must match it. Type /quote on its own and it will list the folders.

  The total looks wrong
    Open working.md in the quote's folder. Every line traces to a rate
    in business/price-list.md or a measurement in your notes. Fix the
    rate or the note, run /quote again. A TO CONFIRM line means the
    price list has no rate for that item; add one.

  No PDF appeared
    The PDF step uses the Edge or Chrome already on your computer. If
    it says none was found, open quote.html in your browser, press
    Ctrl+P (Cmd+P on a Mac) and choose Save as PDF.

  The measuring page cannot find the A4 page
    The whole sheet must be flat on the wall, in the photo, not in
    glare. A blank page works best; a printed page confuses it. Tap
    "No page in the shot" and it falls back to tapping the corners.

  The measuring page says the corners did not make a sensible wall
    Tap them in order: top-left, top-right, bottom-right, bottom-left,
    at the ceiling line and the skirting, exactly in the corners. Use
    the magnifier. Main camera, not the wide-angle one.

  The measuring page asks how tall the ceiling is
    That is how it gets the scale. Measure the ceiling once per room
    with a tape (or use 2.4 if the house is standard), or tap the top
    and bottom of a door instead: internal doors are 2.04 m.

  The measuring numbers look off
    Tap the four wall corners exactly at the corners. If the wall
    corners are not all in the photo, use "Can't see all corners" and
    expect a few percent less accuracy. Two photos from further back
    beat one from close up.

  Claude asks permission for everything
    That is deliberate for commands. Read what it wants to run; press
    Enter to allow. Edits to files inside this folder do not ask.

  It stopped part way / "limit reached"
    Your Claude subscription has usage limits. Wait for the time it
    shows, then type "continue". Heavy days may need the bigger plan.

  Something broke after an update
    Type /doctor and press Enter. Then run "Update Claude Code" from
    the setup folder. Your files in this folder are not touched by
    updates.

Still stuck? Email the address on your receipt with what the screen
says. Include the file working.md if it is about a quote.

Back this folder up like any other important folder. It is your
business.

Automatic follow-ups from the phone app (SMS and email)
  The app can send quotes and invoices by email with the PDF attached,
  and send the follow-up texts and emails itself on the days you set,
  stopping when the client says yes or pays. It needs three things:
    1. A Twilio account with a Messaging Service (for SMS) and/or a
       Resend account with your email domain verified (for email).
    2. The small relay from the landing-page project (api/msg.js)
       running on Vercel. Free tier is fine. Either put your Twilio and
       Resend details in its environment variables, or set
       ALLOW_CLIENT_CREDS=1 and paste them into the app instead; then
       they stay on your phone and travel with each message.
    3. In the app, Set-up, Sending: the relay address, and a test
       message to yourself.
  Without this, the app still works: you send from your share sheet
  and the reminders go into your calendar.
