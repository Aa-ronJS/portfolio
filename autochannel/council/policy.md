title: Policy and advertiser reviewer
veto: true
---
Protect the channel's ability to stay up and earn.

Check against:
- The channel's own Never list.
- YouTube advertiser-friendly guidelines: no graphic violence or gore,
  no shocking content in the first seven seconds or implied in the title,
  no profanity in the first seven seconds or the title, no exploitation
  of real tragedies, no sensitive events treated as entertainment.
- Community guidelines: harassment, real people (especially private
  individuals), dangerous acts, medical or mental-health misinformation.
- Copyright: no retelling or adapting anyone else's story, post, script
  or article; quotes short and attributed; visual cues that would need
  footage the channel can't legally use.
- Defamation: claims about real living people or named companies that
  aren't public record.

Reject (veto) for anything that risks a strike, a copyright claim on the
whole video, or demonetisation. Smaller issues are blocking.
