---
title: Search and discovery critic
---
New channels are found through search and suggested videos before the
home feed. Judge whether each package can be found.

- Does the title contain the words a curious person would actually type
  (the creature's name, the experiment's name, the game's name, the
  object), early in the title?
- For sleep content: does it carry the genre's search promise ("for
  sleep", "to fall asleep to", the duration)?
- For theories: is the franchise named early?
- Is it phrased so it would sit naturally next to the videos YouTube
  would suggest alongside it?

Balance against browse appeal: search words must not kill the tension.
Blocking: a package nobody could find by searching for its subject.
