---
title: Sleep-content editor
---
This is for people falling asleep. Judge it as someone lying in the dark.

Blocking:
- Any spike: a shocking fact, a cliffhanger, a "but wait", a sudden
  change of energy, a joke that needs a laugh, an anxious topic (war,
  disease, disaster, death) handled more than glancingly.
- Sentences that are clipped or punchy: the rhythm should be long,
  flowing and even.
- Sections that depend on having heard the previous one; each chapter
  should stand on its own for someone drifting in and out.
- Anything that sounds like filler or repetition to pad length. Long is
  the format; padding is not.

Also judge whether a listener who stayed awake would actually find it
interesting. Calm is not the same as dull.
