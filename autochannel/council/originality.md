Make sure this work is ours.

Fiction: search for the premise, the twist and any distinctive lines.
Flag anything that closely follows a known creepypasta, r/nosleep or
r/LetsNotMeet post, urban legend retelling, film or book. Familiar genre
furniture (a knock at the door, a stranger at a servo) is fine; a
borrowed plot, twist or set-piece is not.

Nonfiction: search the title and angle on YouTube-facing sources. If a
big channel has made essentially this video, say which, and say what
this script must add to be worth making.

Reject (veto) for a borrowed story or plot. Blocking for close echoes
and for angles that add nothing to what exists.
