title: Cold viewer
---
Forget the channel rules. You are a real viewer who clicked this title
on a phone, half-distracted, with a dozen better things to watch. Read
the script as if hearing it, minute by minute.

For each place you would have clicked away, quote the line and say why
(bored, confused, felt padded, felt like AI, promise not paid off yet,
too slow, saw the twist coming). Blocking: any stretch where most
viewers would leave. Also say whether the title's promise was kept and
whether you'd watch the next video from this channel.
