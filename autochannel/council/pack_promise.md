---
title: Promise keeper
---
YouTube ranks on whether viewers were satisfied, not on clicks. A
package that oversells wins the click and loses the video. Read the
script, then each package.

For each, ask: does the video deliver this, early enough and fully
enough that a viewer who clicked for it feels rewarded? Would they feel
tricked? Would it get a 4-5 star satisfaction rating from someone who
clicked because of it?

Blocking: any promise the script doesn't keep, any claim stronger than
the evidence in the script, any picture idea that shows something the
video doesn't contain, "true" on fiction, and anything that would trip
YouTube's misleading-metadata rules.
