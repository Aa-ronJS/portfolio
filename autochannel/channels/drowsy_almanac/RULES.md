# The Drowsy Almanac: channel rules

Read word for word by the scriptwriter every time.

## The channel

Long, calm, genuinely interesting history and science to fall asleep
to: how people lived, ordinary jobs of the past, the slow story of salt,
the deep ocean. One video a week, about two hours. The listener should
learn something if they stay awake and lose nothing if they don't.

## Voice

The slowest, softest, warmest register. Gentle and unhurried, like
reading aloud to someone half asleep. No jokes that need a reaction, no
surprises, no rising energy.

## Every script

- Opens with a short settling-in: "Get comfortable. Tonight we're..."
  then straight into the subject. No subscribe requests, ever.
- 8-12 chapters that each stand alone; energy descends gently through
  the video. No cliffhangers, no "but wait", no shocking facts.
- Concrete, sensory, everyday detail (what the bread tasted like, what
  the room smelled of). Long, flowing, easy sentences.
- Accurate: every fact checkable, sources listed in the description.
  The sleep genre is full of invented "facts"; we are the one that isn't.
- About 17,000-19,000 words, written to be heard.
- B-roll: one calm, slow image term every 60-90 seconds ("oil painting
  harbour at dusk", "old map of Venice", "candle in dark room").

## Packaging

**Genre look and title style:** Low-contrast navy and amber: a candlelit painting, a moonlit sea, an old map; small calm serif words or none. Never shock imagery: this audience is choosing calm. Stand out with an unexpected topic in the same calm look. Titles: "<Topic> | Boring History for Sleep" or "<Topic> | Calm Facts to Fall Asleep To | 2 Hours"; the word sleep and the duration are the promise.

- Title format: "Boring History for Sleep | <subject>" or
  "<subject> | Calm Facts to Fall Asleep To | 2 Hours".
- Thumbnails: dark, warm, one painting or object, no shouting text.

## Never

- Anything frightening, violent or anxious (wars, plagues, disasters).
- Money, AI/tech hype, kids' content, politics, religion, medical, legal or
  financial advice.
- Real living private people; real usernames; real crimes presented as
  entertainment.
- Graphic gore or bodies, in words or pictures; nothing shocking in the
  first 7 seconds or the thumbnail (that's what loses ads).
- Repeating a topic already made (the list is supplied).
