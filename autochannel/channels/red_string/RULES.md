# Red String Files: channel rules

Read word for word by the scriptwriter every time.

## The channel

Theories about games, films and TV: hidden timelines, what an ending
really meant, the detail everyone missed. Built like a case file:
question, evidence, the problem with the evidence, verdict. For fans who
like to argue in the comments. 15-18 minutes.

## Voice

Brisk, wry detective. Enjoys being clever, enjoys being wrong even more.
Takes fiction seriously without taking itself seriously. Never says
"mind-blowing", "insane" or "you've been watching it wrong".

## Every script

- Hook: the question and the single most surprising piece of evidence,
  inside 20 seconds.
- Evidence beats, each one citing exactly where it comes from (episode,
  scene, level, line of dialogue, interview with the creator). Quote
  dialogue exactly or not at all.
- "But here's the problem": argue against yourself at least once.
- Verdict: say how confident we are, honestly. "Probably", not "proven".
- Separate canon from theory clearly. Never present a theory as fact.
- Only franchises with a real fan base and enough lore to argue about.
- About 2,500 words, written to be heard.
- **Visuals are an evidence board, not footage.** B-roll terms describe
  cards the robot can build: "text card: quote", "timeline: 1998-2004",
  "map: the town", "diagram: family tree", or plain mood stock ("old
  arcade", "rainy window"). Never ask for clips, trailers or character
  images from the work itself.

## Packaging

- Title in the genre's own style (examples in IDEAS.md), under 70
  characters. Curious, not dishonest: the video must deliver what the
  title promises.
- Three genuinely different title/thumbnail ideas; thumbnail text 1-4
  words that add to the title.

## Never

- Leaks, piracy, or spoilers for anything released in the last 30 days
  without a spoiler warning in the first 10 seconds.
- Real actors' private lives; claims about real people beyond public interviews.
- Money, AI/tech hype, kids' content, politics, religion, medical, legal or
  financial advice.
- Real living private people; real usernames; real crimes presented as
  entertainment.
- Graphic gore or bodies, in words or pictures; nothing shocking in the
  first 7 seconds or the thumbnail (that's what loses ads).
- Repeating a topic already made (the list is supplied).
