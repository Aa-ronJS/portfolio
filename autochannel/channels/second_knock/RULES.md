# The Second Knock: channel rules

Read word for word by the scriptwriter every time.

## The channel

Scary stories in the style people love from r/nosleep and r/LetsNotMeet:
first-person, ordinary settings (night shifts, road trips, new
neighbours, camping), slow dread, one moment that makes you check the
door. **Every story is original fiction written for this channel.**
We never retell, adapt or borrow anyone else's post. Each video is 3-4
stories (or one long one) linked by a theme, about 20 minutes.

## Voice

Low, close, unhurried, like someone telling you this at 2am who'd rather
not be. Lets silences do the work. Never shouts, never does voices,
never says "spooky", "creepy pasta" or "smash that like button".

## Every script

- Open cold inside the first story, mid-moment, within one sentence. No
  channel intro. A one-line theme intro is allowed after the first story.
- First person, past tense, specific and mundane details (the brand of
  servo coffee, the song on the radio) that make it feel real.
- Dread over gore: what's implied, glimpsed, wrong. The scariest thing is
  never fully shown. Violence stays off the page.
- Each story turns once, late, and ends without over-explaining.
- No real usernames, no real named towns tied to real crimes, no real
  people. Invented places, or vague real ones ("a highway outside Dubbo").
- The narration never claims the stories are true and never says
  "this really happened" in its own voice. Characters may.
- About 3,000 words total, written to be heard.
- B-roll: one plain atmospheric search term every 6-8 seconds (night
  road, motel corridor, porch light, pine forest fog).

## Packaging

- Title in the genre's own style (examples in IDEAS.md), under 70
  characters. Curious, not dishonest: the video must deliver what the
  title promises.
- Three genuinely different title/thumbnail ideas; thumbnail text 1-4
  words that add to the title.
- Titles never say "true". "Scary", "disturbing", "unsettling" are fine.
- The description starts: "Original fiction. All stories, people and
  events are invented."

## Never

- Money, AI/tech hype, kids' content, politics, religion, medical, legal or
  financial advice.
- Real living private people; real usernames; real crimes presented as
  entertainment.
- Graphic gore or bodies, in words or pictures; nothing shocking in the
  first 7 seconds or the thumbnail (that's what loses ads).
- Repeating a topic already made (the list is supplied).
