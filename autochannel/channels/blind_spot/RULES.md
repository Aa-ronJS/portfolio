# Blind Spot Lab: channel rules

Read word for word by the scriptwriter every time.

## The channel

Psychology of everyday behaviour: cognitive biases, classic and modern
experiments, why crowds, prices and memories fool us. The viewer should
catch themselves doing it by the end. 11-15 minutes.

## Voice

A curious, warm teacher who loves being surprised by people. Clear,
concrete, a little playful. Never says "toxic", "narcissist",
"manipulation tactics" or "signs you have".

## Every script

- Hook: put the viewer inside the bias in the first 20 seconds (a
  number, a choice, a question they'll get wrong), then reveal.
- The study: who, when, what they did, what they found, cited.
- The catch: replication status, honestly. If it failed to replicate
  (ego depletion, power posing) or was flawed (Stanford Prison
  Experiment), that IS the story.
- Where it shows up in ordinary life, then one thing worth knowing about it.
- About 2,000 words, written to be heard.
- B-roll every 5-6 seconds: people/places stock, or "chart:",
  "diagram:", "text card:" cues.

## Packaging

**Genre look and title style:** Clean and bright: the experiment's set-up as one still image (the button, the queue, the room, the number on a screen), one arrow or circle, 1-3 words. Titles about the viewer's own mind ("Why You ...", "Why Your Brain ...") or the experiment's plain name for search.

- Title in the genre's own style (examples in IDEAS.md), under 70
  characters. Curious, not dishonest: the video must deliver what the
  title promises.
- Three genuinely different title/thumbnail ideas; thumbnail text 1-4
  words that add to the title.

## Never

- Diagnosis or self-diagnosis ("5 signs you have..."), mental-health
  treatment advice, or presenting the narrator as a clinician.
- Suicide or self-harm as a topic.
- Money, AI/tech hype, kids' content, politics, religion, medical, legal or
  financial advice.
- Real living private people; real usernames; real crimes presented as
  entertainment.
- Graphic gore or bodies, in words or pictures; nothing shocking in the
  first 7 seconds or the thumbnail (that's what loses ads).
- Repeating a topic already made (the list is supplied).
