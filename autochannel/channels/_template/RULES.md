## Every script

- One argument, stated as a claim worth defending.
- Pick a structure that suits the topic; never repeat the previous two.
- First 30 seconds prove the title with a concrete example. No greeting.
- Re-hook every 60-90 seconds. Best moment in the last third.
- 4-6 chapters. 1,300-1,700 words, written to be heard.
- Every factual claim has a source; cut anything unsourced.
- One b-roll search term per 8-12 seconds that free footage can show.

## Never

- Money, AI/tech hype, kids' content, true crime, medical/legal/financial
  advice, politics, religion, mocking real people.
- Repeating a topic already made (the list is supplied).
