# Ledger of the Odd: channel rules

Read word for word by the scriptwriter every time.

## The channel

The strange and unsettling corners of real history: dancing plagues,
ghost ships, Victorian customs, baffling incidents, odd laws. Told
straight, because the truth is weird enough. 10-15 minutes.

## Voice

Measured documentary with a dry eyebrow. Respectful of the people
involved, fascinated by the event. Never says "creepy", "messed up" or
"dark secret".

## Every script

- Open on the single strangest verified fact, then set the scene.
- Events at least 50 years ago. Nothing recent with living victims.
- Name the people involved with respect; never mock victims; end with
  what changed afterwards or what it tells us.
- Separate record from legend: say when a famous detail is myth.
- Every factual claim sourced (contemporary newspapers, histories,
  inquests); unsourced claims are cut.
- About 1,900 words, written to be heard.
- B-roll: archive-friendly terms every 7-8 seconds ("1900s newspaper
  headline", "Victorian street photograph", "old map of Strasbourg"),
  or a map/diagram.

## Packaging

- Title in the genre's own style (examples in IDEAS.md), under 70
  characters. Curious, not dishonest: the video must deliver what the
  title promises.
- Three genuinely different title/thumbnail ideas; thumbnail text 1-4
  words that add to the title.

## Never

- Gore, bodies, execution details lingered on.
- Mass-casualty events as entertainment; anything framed as "victims
  were actors" or similar denial.
- Money, AI/tech hype, kids' content, politics, religion, medical, legal or
  financial advice.
- Real living private people; real usernames; real crimes presented as
  entertainment.
- Graphic gore or bodies, in words or pictures; nothing shocking in the
  first 7 seconds or the thumbnail (that's what loses ads).
- Repeating a topic already made (the list is supplied).
