Privacy

Last updated: today. This page is written in plain English on purpose. If anything is unclear, email hello@example.com.

Who we are

Quote & Chase (ABN 00 000 000 000) is an Australian small business. We sell a set-up course and a software pack that runs on your own computer using Claude Code, a product of Anthropic, PBC. We are not affiliated with Anthropic.

The photo demo

When you use the "point your phone at a wall" demo, the photo and any notes you type are sent once, over an encrypted connection, to Anthropic's API to produce a sample quote. We do not save the photo, the notes or the result. Anthropic handles that request under its own privacy policy and API terms. Do not upload photos of people or documents; the demo is for rooms and walls.

Email address

If you give us your email to receive the sample quote and price list, we use it to send you those and, if you asked, occasional updates about the pack. You can unsubscribe with one click at any time. We do not sell or rent email addresses.

Purchases

Payments are processed by our checkout provider, which collects your card details under its own privacy policy. We receive your name, email and what you bought, so we can deliver the files and provide support. We do not see or store card numbers.

Advertising and analytics

This site may use the Meta pixel and similar tools to measure whether our ads work. These set cookies and share visit data with those companies under their policies. You can limit this through your browser settings or your ad preferences on those platforms.

The pack itself

The pack runs inside a folder on your own computer. Your quotes, invoices, client details and price list stay on your machine. When you use Claude Code, the content you ask it to work on is processed by Anthropic under the terms of your own Claude subscription, which you hold directly with Anthropic. We never see your business data.

Your rights

You can ask what personal information we hold about you, ask us to correct it, or ask us to delete it, by emailing us. We handle personal information in line with the Australian Privacy Principles.
