How the messages work

What counts as a message, and how far 150 goes.
  • One message is one text or one email the app sends for you. A quote going out, a nudge to someone who has gone quiet, a reminder on an invoice past its date. Anything you send yourself from your own phone is free and always will be.
  • A quote chased to the end costs four. The quote itself, then three nudges. Most are accepted or declined before the third, and a nudge that never has to go is never counted.
  • A reminder holds a message when you book it, and gives it back if it is cancelled. Mark the invoice paid and the ones still queued come back to you.
  • 150 a month is about thirty jobs quoted and chased. The app shows what is left on the Follow-ups tab, so it is never a surprise.
  • Run out and nothing breaks. One tap buys another 100 on the card you are already paying with, or you can have it do that by itself, at most three times in a month. Leave it and the app just writes every message for you to send, the way you do now.

Questions

What does it cost, really?

$99 a month with 150 messages in it, and your first 5 messages free with nothing but an email. A message is one text or one email the app sends for you; anything you send yourself from your own phone costs nothing and never will. Go over in a busy month and a pack of 100 is $35, one tap inside the app. Card payments on invoices are optional and cost Stripe's usual cut, paid to Stripe, not to me.

What's the catch?

There is one price and it is on this page. Nothing is upsold to you, there is nobody to sit through a call with, and cancelling takes two taps inside the app with no notice period. The honest small print: your jobs live on your phone, so you tap Back up when it asks, and the app cannot see your bank account, so you mark invoices paid yourself or it keeps chasing.

How accurate is the measuring?

With any A4 sheet on the wall and the whole wall in the photo, within about 6 cm on a 4 m wall (about one and a half percent). No sheet handy? Tap the corners and scale from a door or the ceiling height, which is a few percent. With an iPhone Pro scan, a centimetre or two. Typed sizes are what you typed, and the quote says who measured.

Does it chase people by itself?

With the subscription on, yes: three nudges for a quote gone quiet and three for an invoice past its date, in your words, on weekdays between 8am and 6pm, never on your state's public holidays. It stops the moment a quote is accepted or you mark an invoice paid. Free, it writes the same messages and you tap Send. Either way nothing goes to a customer until you have tapped Start on that job.

Why is there a monthly price at all?

Because the texts and emails cost real money to send and somebody has to run the thing that sends them. That is what the messages in your plan are. The alternative, which plenty of software does, is to make you open a Twilio account and a Resend account and wire them up yourself; you would pay them instead of me, and you would spend an evening on it. This way it is already built in and you never see it.

Do I have to open accounts with anyone?

No. The texting and emailing are part of the app and come out of your messages. The only optional one is card payments on your invoices, which use a Stripe account in your name because that is your money going into your bank.

What does your sending system see?

The messages you send and the number or email they go to, which pass through my relay and sit in the text and email providers' logs for up to 90 days, as any sending service logs them. I do not see your quotes, prices or jobs; those never leave your phone. I read a message only to fix one that failed, and your log is deleted when you switch over or leave. The privacy page has the list.

What if I want my money back?

Email me inside 30 days of your first payment and I refund that month, no reason needed, whether or not you have cancelled. After that, cancel from inside the app and you are not charged again; I do not refund a month you have already used unless the sending did not work, in which case I do. The wording is here.

Is there anyone to talk to?

By email, and it is me, not a helpdesk: same business day if a message of yours did not send, within two business days for anything else. There is no number to ring and nothing to book, because the whole point is that none of this needs it. If you would rather be talked through something, say so in an email and I will phone you.

Do I need to be good with computers?

For the app, no. Set-up is your name, your bank details and a look at five prices. If you can send a text, you can quote with it. Turning the chasing on takes the technical bits off you entirely: the app arrives already yours from one link you tap, the sending runs through my system, and you type your bank details and nothing else. A phone is enough; there is nothing to install and no password to keep.

Will my customers know it's a free app?

No. The quote is a three-page PDF with your name, licence, insurance, colour schedule and terms. Nothing on it says Quote & Chase. While your sending runs through my system, emails go out from my address with your business name as the sender and replies to your inbox, and texts come from my number signed with your name and mobile; neither says Quote & Chase. Open the sample and check.

What if a customer replies to a text?

Texts sent through my system come from a number that is not yours. Every one says replies to that number are not read and gives them a way back to you: your mobile, or your email if you have not put a mobile in the app. Anyone who replies to it anyway gets an automatic text sending them back to the number in the message. Emails reply straight to your inbox. Texts you send yourself from the app's share button come from your own phone as always.

Where is my data, and what if I lose my phone or you disappear?

On your phone and nowhere else unless you send something. Save the back-up after every invoice and keep it in your own Drive or iCloud; restore it on the new phone. If this website vanished, the app on your phone keeps working and the set-up you paid for stays done. The privacy page lists what Twilio, Resend, Stripe and Google see when you use them.

Does it work with Xero, MYOB or ServiceM8?

Not directly. It exports invoices and payments as CSV, and every document is a PDF, so your bookkeeper gets what they need.

Is there a laptop version?

There is a laptop pack for Claude Code: type the address, drop in the photos, get the PDF, on a Windows or Mac laptop. It suits painters, or their office, who do the admin at a desk. It needs a Claude subscription from Anthropic and sends nothing; the phone app is the product and does not need it. Painters who already pay for Claude can have it for nothing; email me.

Who is behind this?

One independent Australian small business, not a company with a call centre. Not affiliated with Anthropic, Twilio, Resend, Stripe or Google. The contact email and ABN are in the footer, and a person reads the inbox on business days.

How do I put it on my home screen?

It is a web app, so there is no app store. Open it once in your browser, then add it to your home screen and it behaves like any other app, offline included. Use the same address every time: the app keeps its data per address, so a bookmark to a different copy starts empty.
