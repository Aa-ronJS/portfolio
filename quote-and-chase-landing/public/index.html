Questions

Is it really free, and will it start charging me later?

Yes, and no. The phone app is free with no account, and it stays free: it runs on your phone, not on a server I could put a paywall on. If you turn on automatic texts and emails, the accounts they go through (Twilio and Resend) bill you directly, a few cents a message. Card payments cost Stripe's usual cut. Nothing comes to me unless you book the set-up call.

What's the catch?

There is no monthly one. I make money one way: a one-off call where I set the app up with you. Plenty of painters will do the five prices themselves and never talk to me. The small print is that your data lives on your phone, so you tap Back up after every invoice.

How accurate is the measuring?

With any A4 sheet on the wall and the whole wall in the photo, within about 6 cm on a 4 m wall (about one and a half percent). No sheet handy? Tap the corners and scale from a door or the ceiling height, which is a few percent. With an iPhone Pro scan, a centimetre or two. Typed sizes are what you typed, and the quote says who measured.

Does it chase people by itself?

It remembers for you and writes every follow-up and reminder in your words. On the day you set, it shows you who to chase and you tap Text or Email. If you want them to go by themselves on business days and stop the moment the client accepts or pays, that needs text and email accounts in your name: book the set-up call and we switch it on together, or follow the steps in the app's Help. You can hold or cancel any message, and there is a log of everything sent.

What do I get for $495, and why charge if the app is free?

The Tuesday after our call, you quote from the driveway with your name and licence on it, and the chasing happens without you. To get you there I take your prices on a fifteen-minute call, load your details and your state's deposit rule, type in your open book, run your sending through my system for 90 days so you open nothing, send your first real quote with you, save your first back-up, and stay on email for a month with a second look inside it. About four hours of my time per painter. Set-up is the bit people quit at; doing it with you takes my time, not a file. The app never needs it, and plenty of painters do the five prices themselves.

Do I have to open accounts with Twilio, Resend or Stripe?

No. For the first 90 days your follow-ups go through my system with your business name on them and replies to you. After that you can switch to accounts in your own name on a free twenty-minute call, or stay on mine at $39 a month. Card payments are optional and use your own Stripe, because that is your money; we add it on the call or at your second look if you want it. If my system ever stopped, the app goes back to writing the message and you tapping Send, by itself.

What does your sending system see?

The messages you send and the number or email they go to, which pass through my relay and sit in the text and email providers' logs for up to 90 days, as any sending service logs them. I do not see your quotes, prices or jobs; those never leave your phone. I read a message only to fix one that failed, and your log is deleted when you switch over or leave. The privacy page has the list.
