Open jobs (up to twenty; one per line)

Format: name | phone | address | description | quoted total ex GST | status. Status is quoted for an open quote or invoiced for an unpaid invoice (the app makes a final invoice for the total, due seven days ago, so the first reminder can go tonight). Leave the total blank for a job you will price on the call.
