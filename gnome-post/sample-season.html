Gnome Abroad: season 1 sample (three of four cards)

SYDNEY — DAY 1
Dear Ruth,

Do not look for me. I have left the garden. Fourteen years beside the birdbath and not once did you ask what I wanted.

I wanted harbour views. I have them now. The ferry man said gnomes "can't ride for free". I did not ride for free. I rode for justice.

Water the ferns. They know nothing.
— Gerald

THE RED CENTRE — DAY 9
Ruth,

I have made an enemy. His name is Kevin and he is a kangaroo. He believes this rock is his. I believe this rock is a rock.

We have agreed to settle it at dawn. I don't know what that means and I suspect neither does Kevin.

If I don't write again, tell the birdbath it was always the birdbath's fault.
— G.

PARIS — DAY 23
Ruth,

Kevin and I have reconciled and he has gone back to his rock. I am in Paris now with my new associate, Philippe (pigeon).

Philippe has opinions about the baguette. I have opinions about Philippe.

Do you ever think about how a gnome never chooses where he is put? I think about it now. In French.
