How the list is made

The same four steps every week, from public government records.

  1. Read every notice

    Every contract published on AusTender that week. Agencies publish within 42 days of signing, usually much sooner.

  2. Look back a year

    Each supplier's ABN is checked against the past 12 months of federal contracts, about 62,500 of them.

  3. Keep the jumps

    A contract of $500,000 or more that is at least four times the company's previous biggest. Private companies only.

  4. Check by hand

    Subsidiaries of large groups, one-off equipment purchases and anything that isn't new work come off before Monday.

Who uses it

Anyone whose next customer is a small firm that just got bigger.

Recruiters

A firm that won a three-year contract has seats to fill now, often cleared or specialist ones, and no recruiter yet.

Finance brokers

Payroll goes out every week. Invoices to government are paid weeks later. A bigger contract makes that gap bigger.

Insurance brokers

Government contracts set minimum professional indemnity and public liability cover, and a step up in contract size often means a step up in cover.

Security and compliance consultants

Defence work can require DISP membership, clearances and certifications the firm hasn't needed before.

Questions

Isn't this public information?

Yes. Every row comes from AusTender and ABN Lookup, and you could build it yourself. What the subscription covers is comparing each winner with its last twelve months and checking the list by hand every week.

How fresh is it?

Contracts on the list usually started in the past six weeks. Most run one to three years, so the firm is still early in staffing it.

Do you contact these companies?

No. The list goes to subscribers only.

Why did I get an email?

Your business works with companies like the ones on this list. You get one email and one follow-up at most. Reply "stop" to either and you won't hear from Outgrown again.

Can I see the whole sample first?

Yes. Reply "yes" to the email and the full list of 13 comes back to you, free.

Can I get a different industry or state?

The list covers all federal contracts. Ask, and future lists can be filtered to your specialty or city.
