## Structural shifts, 2025 to 2026

**US de minimis is dead; Australia's A$1,000 threshold is intact.** Duty-free
entry for sub-US$800 parcels ended for all origins on 29 August 2025; the flat
postal duty ended 28 February 2026; from 24 July 2026 CBP requires 10-digit HS
codes on everything. Shipping individual parcels from Asia or Australia to US
consumers is now structurally worse. Australia still exempts consignments up
to A$1,000 from duty and border GST, which makes Australia a relatively more
attractive target for offshore sellers and cross-border dropshipping a
relatively worse business for an Australian.

**The US tariff stack on China moved, it did not fall.** The Supreme Court
struck down the IEEPA tariffs on 20 February 2026; a 10 percent Section 122
surcharge ran 24 February to 24 July 2026; USTR then imposed a 12.5 percent
Section 301 duty on all Chinese goods from 24 July 2026, stacking on the
existing 301 lists and MFN. Worked example: made-up textiles at ~28.75
percent effective. Origin, not shipping point, is what matters: a China-made
product posted from Adelaide is China-origin.

**TikTok Shop has not launched in Australia** and TikTok says there is no
imminent rollout (August 2026). Selling on TikTok Shop US requires a US entity,
US bank account and US warehousing with a 48-hour dispatch SLA. Do not build a
launch plan around it.

**Agentic commerce: discovery is real, in-chat checkout died.** OpenAI's
Instant Checkout launched 29 September 2025, was extended to Shopify merchants
on 16 February 2026, and was dropped in March 2026 with fewer than 30 Shopify
merchants live. Since late March 2026 all Shopify stores are in ChatGPT product
discovery by default and purchases redirect to the merchant. AI referrals are
still a low single-digit share of most sites' traffic but grew 200 to 400
percent in a year and convert 42 to 54 percent better than average (Adobe).
ChatGPT is over 80 percent of AI referrals. Sixty-four percent of Australian
consumers use AI for product research. What it requires is structured
product data and quotable pages, which is cheap for a developer and rarely
done at small scale.

**AI Overviews cut organic clicks.** A randomised field experiment (1,065 US
users, early 2026) found AI Overviews on 42 percent of queries cut organic
clicks on those queries by 38 percent; zero-click rose from 54 to 72 percent.
Brands cited inside the overview gain 35 percent organic CTR. Informational
blog SEO is dead for acquisition; product and comparison pages, and being the
cited source, still work.

**Product safety is where Australian regulation is moving.** After a CHOICE
designated complaint, Temu and Gumtree joined Amazon AU, eBay AU and
AliExpress in an ACCC product safety pledge (June 2026). The ACCC sued Amazon
AU (May 2026) and Dusk (July 2026) over button batteries. The ACCC's 2026 to
2027 priorities include greenwashing, fake reviews, drip pricing and
subscription traps. An economy-wide unfair trading practices prohibition is
expected to commence July 2027. A locally accountable seller with a compliance
record is a saleable attribute.

**"Australian made" commands a real but capped premium.** Roy Morgan (May
2025): 72 percent willing to pay more, of whom half accept up to a 10 percent
premium; regional consumers are the most motivated (48 percent).

## Where a software founder has documented leverage

- Email flows produce ~41 percent of email revenue from 5.3 percent of sends
  (Klaviyo 2026, 183,000 brands); email is ~27 percent of e-commerce revenue
  on average. Most stores under A$5M have the automation under-built.
- Zero-party data quizzes and finders: Jones Road captured 124k emails via
  quiz; a size-finder cut returns 95 percent for one brand (vendor-reported).
- Configurators and fitment data lift conversion on customisable products
  (vendor and aggregator claims, 1.1 to 2.8 percent in one study).
- Complete product schema, feeds for Merchant Center, ChatGPT and
  Perplexity, and quotable product pages are six to ten weeks of work most
  brands never do.
- B2B replenishment: involuntary churn is 30 to 40 percent of total and
  recoverable with dunning and smart retries, which is pure engineering.
  Australian B2B buyers want to log in, see their pricing, order, and get a
  tax invoice without a phone call (Stripe AU).
