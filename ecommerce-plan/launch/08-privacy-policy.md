# Privacy policy (draft)

[Legal name] (ABN [number]), trading as [brand], is a small business.

**What we collect.** Business name, ABN, contact name, phone, email and
delivery address; the location of each registered kit (a room, a vehicle
registration); reports made through a kit's QR page (items used, an
optional name, an optional note); AED make, model, serial and expiry dates;
payment records held by our payment provider (Stripe or Shopify), not by us.

**Why.** To supply kits and refills, keep your compliance record, issue
certificates, invoice you, and tell you about renewals and expiries.

address); the automation service that turns scans into our emails. We do
not sell or share your details for marketing. Your compliance record is
reachable by anyone who has its unique link; share the link only with
people you want to see it.

**Marketing.** We only send service messages (renewals, refills, expiries)
unless you opt in to anything else. Every marketing message has an
unsubscribe link, as the Spam Act 2003 requires.

**Access and correction.** Email [email] and we will show you what we hold
and fix anything wrong.

**Security.** Data is stored in Australia on a server we control, backed
up daily, with administrative access limited to us.
