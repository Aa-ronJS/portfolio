# Google Ads: one campaign, South Australia, A$20 a day

The buyer is searching, not browsing. Capture intent; ignore everything
else. Do not touch Performance Max until you have 50 conversions.

## Setup

- Campaign type: Search. Location: South Australia only ("presence", not
  "interest"). Language: English. Budget A$20/day. Bidding: Maximise clicks
  with a A$4 max CPC for the first two weeks, then switch to Maximise
  conversions once the "Account created" conversion has 15 or more.
- Conversion: a Stripe or Shopify purchase, plus a phone-call conversion on
  the call extension. Import the landing-page "Call" click as a secondary.
- Ad schedule: Mon to Fri 6am to 6pm, Sat 7am to 1pm. Tradies search at
  6:30am and at smoko.
- Extensions: call (your mobile), sitelinks (Pricing, How it works, Vehicle
  kits, For clinics), callouts (Same-day refills · Compliance certificate ·
  No visits · Adelaide based), structured snippet (Types: Site kits, Vehicle
  kits, AED pads, Burns modules).

## Ads (responsive search ads, one per ad group)

Headlines (pick 10 to 12 per group; pin the first in position 1):
- First-Aid Kits Kept Compliant
- Refills Ship The Same Day
- Scan The Label, Refill Arrives
- Site & Vehicle Kits, Adelaide
- Compliance Certificate Included
- No Visits, No Forms, No Fuss
- $12/Month Per Kit, Cancel Anytime
- Code Of Practice Contents
- AED Pads Replaced Before Expiry
- Used Something? Just Scan It
- For Trades, Clinics & Childcare
- Adelaide Owned & Operated

Descriptions:
- Every kit gets a QR label. Used something? Scan it and the refill ships
  that day. Scheduled packs twice a year and a certificate on demand.
- Site kits $119, vehicle kits $59, plan from $84 a year per kit. Contents
  aligned to the Safe Work Australia Code of Practice.
- The ute is a workplace too. Vehicle kits registered, tracked and refilled
  without anyone remembering.
- Practice managers and centre directors: the audit trail and certificate
  you need, kept automatically.

## Landing pages

Send everything to the app's landing page (`/`) for now. Within two weeks,
make three variants and route by ad group: `/vehicle` (utes, tradie
language, the $59 + $84 line), `/clinics` (accreditation, ACECQA, the
certificate first), `/aed` (pads and batteries, model list). Each page:
certificate screenshot above the fold, the scan demo, pricing, phone
number, one button.

## What to look at each Monday

Cost, clicks, calls, purchases, cost per account. Pause any keyword with
30 clicks and no call or purchase. Add every search term that converted as
an exact-match keyword; add every irrelevant search term as a negative.
