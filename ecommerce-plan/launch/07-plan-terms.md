# Replenishment plan terms (draft for a lawyer's once-over)

These are plain-language terms written to satisfy the Australian Consumer
Law's expectations on subscriptions (clear price, clear renewal, easy
cancellation, no traps), which the ACCC has named a 2026 to 2027 priority.
Have a lawyer read them before the first annual renewal; publish them on
the site and link them from every invoice.

**1. Who we are.** [Legal name] (ABN [number]), trading as [brand],
Adelaide, South Australia. Contact: [email], [phone].

**2. What you are buying.**
- *Kits* are sold outright. Once paid for and delivered they are yours.
- *The replenishment plan* is a service per registered kit: two scheduled
  refill packs a year replacing dated and sterile items; replacement of
  items you report as used through the kit's QR page (fair use: up to four
  after-use refills per kit per year, more at our published prices); expiry
  tracking; a compliance record and an annual certificate.
- *AED consumables* are supplied and billed when shipped, before the expiry
  date you have recorded for the device.

**3. Price and billing.** Prices are in Australian dollars and exclude GST
unless stated. Annual plans are billed in advance for 12 months. Monthly
plans are billed in advance each month. Prices for renewals may change; we
will tell you at least 30 days before the renewal date.

**4. Renewal.** Annual plans renew automatically for another 12 months on
the renewal date shown on your compliance record unless you cancel before
that date. We send a renewal notice by email at least 30 days before.

**5. Cancellation.** Cancel any time by email or phone. Monthly plans end
at the end of the paid month. Annual plans end at the end of the paid year;
if you cancel within 14 days of first purchase we refund the plan in full.
Cancelling the plan does not affect your ownership of the kits.

**6. What we rely on.** The plan works from what is reported: QR scans,
checks recorded, and expiry dates you give us for AEDs. If nothing is
reported between scheduled packs, we assume nothing was used.

**7. What the plan is not.** We keep kits stocked and keep the record. We do
not assess your first-aid needs, provide first-aid training, or decide
where kits are kept. Those duties remain with the person conducting the
business or undertaking under the Work Health and Safety Act 2012 (SA) and
Regulations. Kit contents follow the example list in the Safe Work
Australia model Code of Practice: First aid in the workplace unless you
choose additional modules; your own risk assessment may require more.

**8. Products.** Kits and consumables are supplied by Australian
manufacturers whose products are listed on the Australian Register of
Therapeutic Goods where required. We do not repackage sterile goods.

**9. Delivery.** Refills reported before 2pm on a business day ship the
same day by Australia Post to the address on your record; allow 2 to 6
business days depending on location.

**10. Consumer guarantees.** Nothing in these terms excludes the guarantees
under the Australian Consumer Law. If a product is faulty we will replace
or refund it.

**11. Privacy.** We collect business contact details, kit locations and
usage reports to deliver the service. We do not sell them. See our privacy
policy at [URL].

**12. Changes.** We may update these terms with 30 days' notice by email.
Continued use after that date is acceptance.
