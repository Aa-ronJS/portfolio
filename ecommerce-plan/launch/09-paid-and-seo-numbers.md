# What paid ads and SEO can actually produce

Written 13 September 2026. Benchmarks are cited; the first-aid keyword
volumes and CPCs for Australia are estimates until you pull them from Google
Keyword Planner (free with the Ads account you open tomorrow). Rerun the
arithmetic with real numbers after week one.

## The constraint nobody budgets for: South Australia is small

SA is about 7 percent of Australia. The buying-intent cluster (restock,
refill, workplace kit, vehicle kit, AED pads, compliance) is a few thousand
searches a month nationally at most, so in SA it is on the order of **150 to
300 searches a month**. At a 6 percent click-through rate on exact-match
ads you cannot buy more than roughly 250 relevant clicks a month in SA no
matter what you spend. A$20 a day is not a timid budget here; it is close
to the ceiling. Budget is not the lever in SA. Conversion and geography are.

## Paid search: the funnel with three cases

Assumptions per case: cost per click, ad click-through rate on intent
terms, landing page to lead (call, form or purchase) rate, lead to account
close rate with you following up by phone, and direct online purchase rate.

| Case | CPC | CTR | Lead rate | Close rate | Direct buy |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Low | A$5.00 | 4% | 3% | 30% | 0.8% |
| Base | A$3.00 | 6% | 5% | 40% | 1.5% |
| High | A$2.00 | 8% | 8% | 50% | 2.5% |

Benchmarks behind them: B2B services search CTR 2.4 to 3.5 percent overall,
higher on exact-match intent terms; B2B services landing conversion 5 to 10
percent, lead generation 7 to 12 percent; B2B CPC about US$3.33; good 2026
search benchmarks are 3 to 5 percent CTR, US$2 to 4 CPC, 3 to 5 percent
conversion (WebFX, 42 Agency, MetricNexus, 2026). A brand new site with no
reviews sits at the low end of those in months one to three.

| Scenario | Budget / month | Clicks | Leads | Accounts | CAC |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| SA, low | A$600 | 120 | 3.6 | 2.0 | A$294 |
| SA, base | A$600 | 200 | 10.0 | 7.0 | A$86 |
| SA, high | A$600 | 250 (capped) | 20.0 | 16.2 | A$37 |
| SA, base, more budget | A$900 | 250 (capped) | 12.5 | 8.8 | A$103 |
| National, low | A$3,000 | 600 | 18.0 | 10.2 | A$294 |
| National, base | A$3,000 | 1,000 | 50.0 | 35.0 | A$86 |
| National, high | A$3,000 | 1,500 | 120.0 | 97.5 | A$31 |

How to read it:

- **Base case in SA is 5 to 9 accounts a month at a CAC under A$100**,
  which is the A$95 the financial model assumes. Paid search alone does not
  build the business in SA; it fills in around the founder-led selling.
- **The low case breaks the model.** At A$294 CAC the day-one self-funding
  property is gone (the model goes to minus A$13.6k for the year at A$200
  CAC). That is why the stop rule exists: two consecutive months over A$150
  and you pause and fix the landing page or the offer before spending more.
- **Going national is where budget starts to matter**, because volume is no
  longer the cap. But close rates fall when you cannot drop a kit in
  person, so use the low case, not the base case, for interstate until the
  self-serve flow has proven itself. Tie the national push to Gate G3.
- **CPC is the assumption you can verify tomorrow.** Keyword Planner will
  show it. If restock and compliance terms are under A$2.50 in Australia,
  paid search is comfortably profitable; if they are over A$5, it is a
  minor channel.

## Which paid channels and which not

| Channel | Expected in this business | Verdict |
|---|---|---|
| Google Search, exact and phrase match | Above | Yes, from week one |
| Google Performance Max | Needs 30 to 50 conversions to learn; spends on display and YouTube you do not want | Not before 50 accounts |
| Meta | AU CPM about A$12 to 18; B2B lead CPA A$40 to 120 for local services, but leads are low intent and the buyer is at work. Learning phase needs about A$10k a month to stabilise | No |
| LinkedIn | CPC A$8 to 15, cost per lead A$100 to 300; only makes sense for multi-site targets like practice managers and childcare groups | Maybe in year two, as a targeted test |
| Amazon AU and eBay AU listings | Free listings; eBay AU has no transaction fees under A$25k a year. Demand capture and review velocity, low volume | Yes, listings only, no ads until 20 reviews |

## SEO: what a new domain can expect

Timelines from 2026 studies: new domains typically sit in a suppressed
period of 3 to 6 months; local service businesses see Google Maps movement
in 4 to 10 weeks with a complete Google Business Profile, consistent
citations and reviews; national informational rankings take 6 to 12 months
and longer in competitive spaces (Search Engine Land, Ahrefs and Semrush data
via FactoryJet, RankAI, 2026).

Two other 2026 facts shape the plan. AI Overviews appear on 42 percent of
queries and cut organic clicks on those queries by 38 percent (randomised
field experiment, 1,065 users, early 2026). Brands cited inside the overview
gain about 35 percent organic click-through. So informational blog traffic
is worth less than it was, and being the cited answer is worth more.

| Phase | Organic sessions a month | Lead rate | Leads | Accounts (40% close) | Founder hours a week |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Months 1 to 3 | ~100 (brand, Maps) | 1.0% | 1 | 0.4 | 2 |
| Months 4 to 6 | ~500 | 1.2% | 6 | 2.4 | 2 |
| Months 7 to 9 | ~1,200 | 1.5% | 18 | 7 | 2 |
| Months 10 to 12 | ~2,500 | 1.5% | 38 | 15 | 2 |

Those are estimates for a site with five to ten well-built answer pages, a
Google Business Profile with 20-plus reviews, and product pages with full
structured data. Organic leads convert lower than paid because the intent
mix includes students and home users; the 1 to 1.5 percent rate reflects
that.

What produces those numbers, in order of return:

1. **Google Business Profile as a service-area business in Adelaide**, day
   one. Category "Safety equipment supplier" or "First aid station". Ask
   every one of the first 20 customers for a review with a link. This is
   what ranks for "first aid kit restock adelaide" in weeks, not months.
2. **Product pages with complete structured data** (Product, Offer, price,
   availability, reviews) so Google Shopping's free listings, ChatGPT
   product discovery and Gemini can all see the kits. Cheap for you, rare
   at this size.
3. **Five to ten answer pages** on the questions buyers actually type:
   what a workplace first-aid kit must contain in SA; how often to check a
   kit; vehicle kit requirements for tradies; AED pad and battery expiry;
   ACECQA first-aid requirements; burns kit requirements for kitchens.
   Short answer first, then the detail, then the offer. One a week.
4. **Comparison pages** by month four: kit restock service versus
   quarterly visit service; buying kits from a hardware store versus a
   plan. Commercial intent survives AI Overviews; informational intent does
   not.
5. **Citations and mentions**: association member directories, supplier
   listings, a couple of local trade publications. These are the links a
   new domain needs and they come with the partner channel anyway.

AI search specifically: 64 percent of Australian consumers use AI for
product research and AI-referred visitors convert 42 to 54 percent better,
but AI referrals are still a low single-digit share of most sites' traffic.
Expect single-digit visits a month from ChatGPT and Gemini in year one. Do
the structured data work because it is nearly free and compounds, and
budget nothing from it.

## The blended picture for year one

| Source of new accounts | Months 1 to 3 | Months 4 to 6 | Months 7 to 12 |
|---|---|---|---|
| Founder-led direct sales and referrals | 70 to 80% | 50 to 60% | 30 to 40% |
| Paid search | 15 to 25% | 20 to 25% | 15 to 20% |
| Partners | 0 | 10 to 20% | 25 to 35% |
| SEO and Maps | under 5% | 5 to 10% | 15 to 20% |

The model's ramp (6 accounts in month one to 46 in month twelve) is
consistent with paid search contributing 5 to 9 a month in SA, SEO reaching
10 to 15 a month by month twelve, and the rest coming from you and from
partners. If paid search comes in at the high case, the ramp is
conservative. If it comes in at the low case, nothing changes except that
you turn it off and the ramp depends more on partners.

## What to measure, and when to change course

Weekly from the Ads account: clicks, calls, purchases, cost per account,
search terms report. Monthly from Search Console and the Business Profile:
impressions, clicks, Maps calls and direction requests, reviews.

Change course when: paid CAC is over A$150 for two consecutive months
(pause, fix, restart); a keyword has 30 clicks and no lead (pause it);
organic sessions are under 300 a month at month six (the answer pages are
not being found; check indexing and titles before writing more); Maps is
not showing the business for "first aid kit adelaide" by week ten (profile
or category problem, fix before anything else).
