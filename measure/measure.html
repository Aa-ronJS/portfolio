Measure a wall

Tape the measure sheet to the wall, take one photo of the whole wall, then tap the corners. Nothing leaves this page.

  1. Print the sheet at 100% size. Open the measure sheet. Check the 100 mm bar with a tape.
  2. Tape it flat on the wall you are measuring, about chest height, anywhere on the wall.
  3. Photograph the whole wall from straight in front, lights on. Stand back so every edge of the wall is in the shot. Main camera, not wide angle.
  4. Tap the corners here: the wall, then each door and window. Two taps each.
