MEASURE A WALL WITH A SHEET OF PAPER

1. Open measure-sheet.html and print it at 100% size. Check the bar
   is 100 mm with a tape.
2. Tape the sheet flat on the wall you want to measure.
3. Take one photo of the whole wall, straight on, lights on. Not the
   wide-angle lens.
4. Open measure.html (double-click it). Choose the photo. Tap the
   top-left and bottom-right corners of the wall, then of each door
   and window. Save the wall. Repeat for other walls or rooms.
5. Save the measurements file into the job folder in quotes/incoming,
   next to the photos. The quote uses those numbers instead of
   estimating.

Expected accuracy: about 1 to 3 percent when the whole sheet is in the
photo and the wall is roughly straight on. The page tells you its
estimate for each wall.

Everything runs inside your browser. No photo leaves your computer.
The marker detector is js-aruco2 (MIT licence), see lib/.
