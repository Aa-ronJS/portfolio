# The Website Kit

## Start here

You bought the kit I hand people instead of a quote. It exists because
about half the website enquiries I get do not need a developer; they
need the decisions made in the right order, the traps named before they
bite, and the exact way to use AI tools to do the building. That is
what this is.

**What you have:** six documents, meant to be read in order the first
time, then used out of order forever.

1. **Start here** (this one): how the kit works, and the promises.
2. **Decide:** what kind of site you actually need, and what it should
   cost you in money and hours. Twenty minutes of reading that saves
   the most money of anything in the kit.
3. **Foundations:** domain, hosting, email. The unglamorous part almost
   everyone gets slightly wrong, done right in an afternoon, owned by
   you forever.
4. **Build:** the main event. Two tracks (WordPress, or an AI-built
   site), the honest way to choose between them, and the prompt pack:
   the actual working method for getting an AI assistant to build,
   fix and explain things for you.
5. **Launch:** words, basic search visibility, and the checklist that
   decides when it is ready. Most DIY sites fail here, not in the
   build.
6. **Keep:** the small maintenance habit that stops your site quietly
   rotting, and the honest list of moments when DIY stops being the
   right call.

**The promises, so you can hold me to them:**

- Nothing is held back. There is no secret step that requires booking a
  call. Where something is genuinely hard, the kit says so and says
  why, instead of hiding it.
- The prompts in document 4 are the real method: the way I instruct AI
  tools in my own work, rewritten for someone who does not code.
- **If you get partway through and decide to hire me for this job
  instead, the kit price comes off the invoice.** The kit is the first
  step either way, so you cannot lose money by having bought it.
- Refunds are simple: if the kit was not worth it, say so within 30
  days and you get your money back. Keep the files.

**The support boundary, stated plainly:** the price bought the
documents. If a step in the kit is wrong or unclear, tell me; that is
a fault in the product and I will fix it and thank you. "Can you just
have a look at my site" is consulting, and the services site is where
that lives, with your kit credit waiting.

**Time and money, honestly, before you start:** a competent, motivated
person following this kit builds a proper small-business site over one
to three weekends, spending typically $150 to $400 a year on domain,
hosting and email, plus modest AI tool subscriptions if you do not
already have one. If the number in your head was "one evening and
free", read document 2 first; it exists for exactly that
conversation.

One more thing: the kit assumes nothing about you technically. Where a
term first appears it gets one plain-words sentence. If a term ever
appears without one, that is a defect; report it.
