## The one-follow-up email (calculator leads)

The gate promises "at most one follow-up". This is that follow-up; send it
once, personally, within a day or two, then stop unless they reply.

> Subject: Your [service] estimate ([range])
>
> Hi [name],
>
> You ran my calculator over a [service] yesterday and got [range]. Two
> things worth knowing beyond what the page could say:
>
> [One genuinely useful, specific observation based on their answers, e.g.
> "You picked 'migration from an existing store': the part that decides
> cost there is usually the URL and ranking carry-over, and it's also the
> part cheap quotes skip."]
>
> If it's worth a conversation, Wednesdays 11:30 Adelaide time is my slot:
> [booking link]. If the timing's wrong or the range killed it, no reply
> needed and you won't hear from me again.
>
> Aaron
