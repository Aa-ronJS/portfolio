Fair questions

Before you ask.

Why is my WordPress site so slow?
Usually some mix of cheap hosting, a heavy theme, too many plugins doing overlapping jobs, and images uploaded straight off a phone. The fix starts with measuring which of those it actually is, then removing causes instead of stacking a caching plugin on top of the pile.
My site was hacked. Can it be cleaned?
Almost always, yes: isolate it, rebuild from known-good code and a clean database export, rotate every credential, close the way in. Sometimes a rebuild is cheaper than a cleanup, and you will get that comparison honestly before spending anything.
Do I really need a page builder?
Rarely. Page builders trade speed and maintainability for drag-and-drop convenience, and most business sites change too infrequently to collect on that trade. Where you genuinely need self-service editing, it gets set up deliberately, for the parts you actually edit.
What does a rebuild cost?
It depends on how much content survives, how much design is wanted, and what the old site is hiding. That is why the quote is fixed and written after I have seen the site, rather than a price list that turns into an argument later. The quote costs nothing.

What I can show publicly lives on my portfolio: live tools with public source code and a platform build you can compile. Client sites get shared privately in a conversation, with their owners' blessing, which is also how I would treat yours.
