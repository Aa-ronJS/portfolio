Fair questions

Before you ask.

Our numbers do not match between systems.
My favourite kind of job. The method is the same at any size: trace the discrepancy to its cause, explain it in plain words, repair the records that matter, reconcile against a source you trust, then automate the join so it stays right.
Do we need a BI tool, or is a spreadsheet enough?
A spreadsheet is enough more often than the BI industry would like you to know, and a well-built one beats a dashboard nobody opens. The honest test is who reads it, how often, and from where. You get that recommendation before any tool gets bought.
Someone builds the same report by hand every week.
The most automatable hour in most businesses. Pull from the sources directly, transform once, deliver on schedule, keep every number traceable. The person gets their Friday back and the report stops depending on their memory.
Can AI help here?
Yes, carefully: summarising what moved and why in plain language, flagging anomalies worth a human's attention, tidying data as it arrives. What it must never do is invent a number, which is why everything here stays traceable to source. More at AI development.
