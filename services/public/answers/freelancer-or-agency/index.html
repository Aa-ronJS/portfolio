Freelancer or agency: which should we hire?

An agency buys you a team and its overheads; a senior freelancer buys you one accountable brain. For most small business work, one senior person with AI leverage now delivers what previously justified the agency, without the account manager between you and the person building.

The longer answer

In practice.

What the agency premium buys

Capacity in parallel, cover when someone is sick, process, and a brand to sue. Genuinely worth it for large builds needing five specialists at once. The premium is real: you fund the office, the juniors and the pitch deck.

What changed recently

AI moved the line. Scope that needed a team now fits one senior person who specifies, builds and checks, per how that works, so the agency-sized budget buys freelancer speed twice over, or stays in your pocket.

The risk people fear, answered

"What if the freelancer disappears" is solved by ownership, not headcount: everything in your accounts, documented, handoverable. Judge any hire, per judging a developer, by how hard they work to make themselves replaceable.
