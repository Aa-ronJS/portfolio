Is an AI chatbot worth it for a small business?

Worth it when it is wired to your real information and allowed to say "I'll get a human": it answers the questions that fill your inbox at 10pm. Worthless and occasionally harmful when it improvises, because a bot that invents your refund policy costs more than it saves.

The longer answer

In practice.

The version that works

Grounded in your actual prices, availability, policies and documents; honest about uncertainty; escalating anything delicate to a person with the conversation attached. On those terms it genuinely deflects the repetitive majority and captures the after-hours lead.

The version to refuse

A generic widget pointed at your homepage, improvising answers in a confident tone. The failure mode is not "unhelpful"; it is a customer holding a screenshot of a policy you never had.

The economics

Count the repetitive questions per week and what interrupting a human for each costs. Past a handful a day the grounded version pays quickly; below that, a better FAQ page is the honest recommendation, and I make it, per AI development.
