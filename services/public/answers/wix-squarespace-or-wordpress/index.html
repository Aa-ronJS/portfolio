Wix, Squarespace or WordPress: which should a small business use?

If your site is a straightforward brochure and you want to edit it yourself with zero maintenance, the hosted builders are genuinely fine. WordPress earns its keep when you need ownership, integrations, or search performance at scale, and custom builds earn theirs when the site is really an application.

In practice.

The honest case for the builders

Wix and Squarespace bundle hosting, security and editing for a monthly fee, and for a cafe or a consultant's card-on-the-web they are often the right call. A developer who cannot say that is selling you their preference.

Where they run out

Deep integrations with your CRM or job system, serious multi-page search strategies, exporting your site if you ever want to leave, and costs that look small monthly and large over five years. Businesses outgrow builders quietly and then feel stuck.

How to choose

Pick for the next three years, not the next month: what must the site connect to, who edits it, how much does search matter. I build on both sides of this line, per WordPress and custom work, so the recommendation follows your answer, not my toolbox.
