Fair questions

Before you ask.

Shopify or WooCommerce?
Shopify when you want hosting, security and checkout carried for you and are happy paying monthly for that. WooCommerce when you want ownership, are already on WordPress, or need something Shopify's model fights. I work in both, so the recommendation follows your business, not my preference.
Orders come in, then everything is manual.
The highest-value fix in most stores: orders flowing into shipping labels, invoices into accounting, stock levels back to the site, exceptions surfaced to a human. Automating whole processes end to end is most of what I do; see CRM and automation.
The store is slow and conversions are poor.
Start by measuring, not guessing: speed with the same tools Google uses, then the checkout path step by step. Most slow stores are carrying apps nobody remembers installing; most leaky checkouts lose buyers at one or two specific points. Fix causes, measure again, show you both numbers.
Can you migrate us between platforms?
Yes, and the dangerous part is not the theme, it is the data: products, customers, order history and the URLs Google already ranks. Migrations here get a mapping, a rehearsal and a reconciliation, the same discipline I bring to CRM migrations, because it is the same job.
