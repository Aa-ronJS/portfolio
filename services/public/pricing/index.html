What would yours cost?

Answer a few plain questions and get an honest range on the spot, built from typical Australian market pricing for work shaped like yours. It is an estimate, not a quote: the quote is fixed, in writing, after a short conversation, and it stays inside what you can hold me to.

How the numbers work

Honest, or not worth gating.

  • The ranges are market-typical, assembled from what work shaped like yours commonly costs in Australia from an independent senior developer, and kept wide on purpose: your specifics narrow them, and only a conversation earns a single number.
  • One person with AI leverage sits at the friendly end of those ranges more often than not, because you are not funding an office, an account manager and a pitch deck.
  • Your details go to me, once. No list, no automation sequence, no reselling. At most one follow-up email or call, then silence unless you want more.
  • Sometimes the honest output is "don't spend it": several paths in this calculator will tell you a cheaper option fits better, because that is what I would say on the phone.
