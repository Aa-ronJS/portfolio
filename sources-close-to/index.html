Questions from the floor.

Will it be mean?
No. The newsroom roasts habits, never the person. Nothing about weight, health, appearance, money or anything that reads as genuinely painful. Cruel tips get left out. The last page is sincere.
Who writes it?
An editor built on Claude, working from the tips only. It invents sources, correspondents and fake products. It does not invent facts about real people. You read it before anyone else does, and one rewrite is included.
What do I get?
Six A4 pages in your browser: print them at home, or Save as PDF and take it to Officeworks for a glossy version. Print as many as the party needs.
Can the birthday person see the tips?
Only if someone sends them the link. The tip line and the finished issue are on private links. Keep them in the chat they are not in.
How long does it take?
Opening the tip line: thirty seconds. Collecting: as long as you give it. Writing the issue: about a minute after you pay.
Refunds?
If the issue does not render or something in it is wrong and you tell me within a week, the money comes back. Reply to the receipt.
