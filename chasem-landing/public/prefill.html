Prices (leave blank to keep the app's defaults or the painter's own)

One per line as key = number. Keys are the app's price keys (open the app, Set-up, Prices, and hover a field, or export a back-up and read the prices block). Common ones: p_walls, p_ceiling, p_skirting, p_door, p_window, p_prep_hour, p_setup, p_weatherboard, p_render, p_eaves, p_gutters.

Open jobs (up to twenty; one per line)

Format: name | phone | address | description | quoted total ex GST | status | due or sent. Status is quoted for an open quote or invoiced for an unpaid invoice (the app makes a final invoice for the total). The last column is the real date the invoice fell due, or the quote went out: 2026-08-30, or a number of days ago such as 12; blank means three weeks ago. The app queues nothing when the painter loads it; on the call he taps Start the chasing, and the first reminder on anything already overdue goes the next morning, the rest on their dates. Leave the total blank for a job you will price on the call.
