1. Set up your app

Getting your set-up link…

Set up my app

Open this on the phone you quote from and tap Load. Your business name, ABN, licence and your state's deposit rule go in, and your month's messages are on it from that moment. The same link is in your email, so you can do it on the ute tomorrow instead.

Your set-up link is in the welcome email, arriving now at the address you paid with. Open it on the phone you quote from and tap Load. Not there in an hour? Email me and I will send it again.

Open it on the phone you actually quote from. The app keeps everything on that phone, so the link is worth nothing to anyone else and it is the only set-up there is.

2. Five minutes on your prices, whenever suits

The app starts with middle-of-the-road Australian rates so you can quote tonight. To make them yours it asks one question, what you charge for a day on the tools, and works the whole list out from that. Or give it a room you have already quoted and it moves every price to match. It is the second thing on the set-up list inside the app.

While you are in there, put your bank details in so your invoices can be paid, and read the three nudges under Set-up. Change any wording that does not sound like you.

3. Anyone who already owes you

Add them as jobs, then tap Start the chasing. Anything already past its date goes out the next morning, the rest on their days. Nothing is sent to a customer before you tap that button, and each one that goes comes off your month's messages.

Your messages, your card, cancelling

All three live inside the app. The Follow-ups tab shows how many messages are left this month. Set-up, then Manage or cancel, opens your own Stripe page to change the card, read receipts, or stop. There is no notice period and no email to send. Cancel and the app keeps working, writing every message for you to send by hand.

Thirty days, money back: if the chasing has not happened the way the website says, email me inside 30 days of your first payment and I refund that month, whether you cancelled or not. The wording.
