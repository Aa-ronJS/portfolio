How the messages work

What counts as a message, and how far 150 goes.
  • One message is one text or one email the app sends for you. A nudge to someone who has gone quiet, a reminder on an invoice past its date. Anything you send yourself from your own phone is free and always will be.
  • A quote chased to the end costs three or four. Up to three nudges, and the invoice reminders if it comes to that. Most are answered before the third, and a nudge that never has to go is never counted.
  • Only the next nudge is ever booked. It books one chase-up, not three. The one after it is booked when that one has gone, so a quote answered in the first week costs one message, not three.
  • A booked reminder holds a message, and gives it back if it is cancelled. Mark the invoice paid and the one already booked comes back to you; the ones still waiting never cost anything at all.
  • 150 a month is about thirty jobs chased. The app shows what is left on the Follow-ups tab, so it is never a surprise.
  • Run out and nothing breaks. One tap buys another 100 on the card you are already paying with. Leave it and the app just writes every message for you to send, the way you do now.

  • Nothing else. No logins to keep, no renewal to remember. Cancel and the app keeps working, writing every message for you to send by hand.

Questions

What does it cost, really?

$99 a month with 150 messages in it, and your first three jobs free — 12 messages — with nothing but an email. A message is one text or one email the app sends for you; anything you send yourself from your own phone costs nothing and never will. Go over in a busy month and a pack of 100 is $35, one tap inside the app. Card payments on invoices are optional and cost Stripe's usual cut, paid to Stripe, not to me.

What's the catch?

There is one price and it is on this page. Nothing is upsold to you, there is nobody to sit through a call with, and cancelling takes two taps inside the app with no notice period. The honest small print: your work is kept in your account as well as on your phone, so a customer can accept and book — the privacy page says exactly what that is. And the app cannot see your bank account, so you mark bank transfers paid yourself or it keeps chasing.

Which trades is it for?

Anyone who sends quotes and invoices to customers: electricians, plumbers, builders, carpenters, tilers, plasterers, roofers, landscapers, concreters, fencers, air-con, handymen, cleaners, gardeners, pest control and painters. Pick your trade when you start, or Something else. The deposit rules for building work only come in for the trades they apply to.

Do I have to stop using my quoting app?

No. Chasem chases what you already send. Export your quotes from Tradify, ServiceM8, Fergus, simPRO, Xero, MYOB or QuickBooks as a spreadsheet and drop the file in, or paste the text you sent. It does not care where the quote was made.

Does it chase people by itself?

With the sending on, yes: up to three nudges for a quote gone quiet and three for an invoice past its date, in your words, on weekdays at 9am, never on your state's public holidays. It stops the moment a quote is accepted or you mark an invoice paid. With the sending off, it writes the same messages and you tap Send. Either way nothing goes to a customer until you have tapped Start on that job.

How does a customer say yes?

They reply YES to the text, or tap the link in the quote to accept it and pick a start day from the days you are free. You see it the next time you open the app, and the deposit invoice is already written.

Why is there a monthly price at all?

Because the texts and emails cost real money to send and somebody has to run the thing that sends them. That is what the messages in your plan are. The alternative is to make you open accounts with a texting company and an email company and wire them up yourself; this way it is already built in and you never see it.

Do I have to open accounts with anyone?

No. The texting and emailing are part of the app and come out of your messages. The only optional one is card payments on your invoices, which use a Stripe account in your name because that is your money going into your bank.

What if I want my money back?

Email me inside 30 days of your first payment and I refund that month, no reason needed, whether or not you have cancelled. After that, cancel from inside the app and you are not charged again. The wording is here.

Is there anyone to talk to?

By email, and it is me, not a helpdesk: same business day if a message of yours did not send, within two business days for anything else. There is no number to ring and nothing to book, because the whole point is that none of this needs it.

Do I need to be good with phones?

No. If you can send a text, you can use it. It opens with your email, the buttons are pictures with a word under them, and set-up is your trade, your name and your bank details.

Will my customers know it's an app?

No. Texts are signed with your name and your mobile, and emails go out under your business name with replies to your inbox. Nothing says Chasem.

What if a customer replies to a text?

Texts sent through my system come from a number that is not yours. Every one gives them a way back to you: your mobile, or your email. A YES is passed straight to the app; anything else gets an automatic text pointing them to your number. Emails reply straight to your inbox.

Where is my data, and what if I lose my phone?

In two places: a full copy on your phone, and your account here. Lose the phone and a new one fills itself back in — except your keys and your PIN, which never leave the handset. Ask and I delete the account and everything in it. The privacy page lists exactly what is held and who sees what.

Who is behind this?

One independent Australian small business, not a company with a call centre. Not affiliated with Tradify, ServiceM8, Fergus, simPRO, Xero, MYOB, QuickBooks, Stripe or Google. The contact email and ABN are in the footer, and a person reads the inbox on business days.

How do I put it on my home screen?

It is a web app, so there is no app store. Open it once in your browser, then add it to your home screen and it behaves like any other app, offline included. Use the same address every time.

iPhone

  1. Open the app in Safari, not inside another app.
  2. Tap Share, the square with the arrow at the bottom.
  3. Scroll down and tap Add to Home Screen, then Add.

Android

  1. Open the app in Chrome.
  2. Tap the three dots at the top right.
  3. Tap Install app (on some phones, Add to Home screen), then Install.
