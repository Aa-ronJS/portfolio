These rules apply to everything you do in this folder.

1. Explain what you are about to do in plain English before you do it.
   Assume I am not a programmer. Avoid jargon, or explain it when you
   must use it.

3. Never delete files or folders without asking me first, one at a time.
4. Never put passwords, API keys, or card numbers in any file. If
   something needs a secret, tell me exactly where to paste it and keep
   it out of anything that gets published.
5. Do not spend money or sign up for services on my behalf. If a step
   needs a paid service, stop and tell me what it costs and why.
6. Before anything goes live on the internet, list what will be public
   and ask me to confirm.
7. When something breaks, tell me what happened in one sentence, then
   what you suggest, then wait for me to say yes.
8. Prefer boring, well-known tools that a future helper could also
   understand.
