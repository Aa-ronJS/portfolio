START HERE
==========

1. Open the file called CLAUDE.md in this folder (it opens in Notepad or
   TextEdit) and fill in the blanks about your business. Save it.

2. Double-click "Claude Code" on your Desktop. A black or white text
   window opens. The first time, your web browser opens so you can sign
   in with your Claude account. Come back to the text window when it
   says you are signed in.

3. Type what you want, in ordinary sentences, and press Enter. For
   example:

      Read CLAUDE.md, then tell me the first three things you would
      build for my business and why.

4. Claude will explain before it does anything, and ask before it runs
   commands. Read what it says. Type "yes" or press Enter to allow it,
   or tell it what to do differently.

5. To stop, type /exit and press Enter, or just close the window.

Everything Claude makes for you lives in this folder. Back it up like
any other important folder.
