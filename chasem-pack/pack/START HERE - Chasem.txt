QUOTE AND CHASE - START HERE
============================

Step one is the phone app, and it is free
  Everything a painter needs is in the phone app: measure a wall from
  one photo, quote at your rates, send the PDF, invoice, take card
  payments, book the days and follow up. It stores everything on your
  phone. Open it in your phone's browser and add it to your home
  screen (iPhone: Share, Add to Home Screen. Android: Chrome menu,
  Install app). The app's own Help screen covers the rest.

This pack is the optional laptop extras
  If you also like doing admin at a desk, this pack gives Claude Code
  three commands for the same job. It is for people who are happy in
  a terminal window; the phone app never needs one. The pack needs a
  Claude subscription (paid to Anthropic); the phone app does not.

  /quote     photos + notes  ->  PDF quote
  /invoice   accepted quote  ->  PDF invoice
  /chase     who owes you    ->  reminder texts and emails, drafted

For laptop users: set up once (about 10 minutes)
  1. Open the "business" folder. Fill in details.md (your business,
     bank details, how you talk to customers). Save.
  2. Open price-list.md. Change every price to yours. Save. Claude only
     ever quotes from this list, so it is worth getting right.
  3. Read quote-wording.md and change anything you would not say.

For laptop users: your first quote (about 5 minutes)
  1. Double-click "Claude Code" on your Desktop.
  2. Type:   /quote Example - 12 Wattle St Ringwood
     and press Enter. Watch what it does. It will read the example
     notes, work out the areas, price them from your list, write the
     PDF and log the job.
  3. Open quotes/sent and look at the PDF. Then open working.md next
     to it and read the assumptions. That is what you check before any
     real quote goes out.

Measure it properly (a minute a wall, worth it, nothing to print)
  1. Stick any A4 sheet flat on the wall, printed or blank. Photograph
     the whole wall, corner to corner, lights on. Expect about one and
     a half percent.
  2. Easiest: do it in the phone app (New job, add a room, Measure
     from photo). On a laptop, open the "measure" folder and
     double-click measure.html, then choose the photo. It outlines the
     page: confirm. It outlines the wall: confirm. Doors and windows it
     can see are listed; add any it missed with two taps. Save.
  3. No page in the shot? Tap the four wall corners and use a door
     (2.04 m) or the ceiling height for the size.
  4. Put the measurements.json it makes in the job folder with the
     photos. The quote then uses real numbers, not estimates.
  Got an iPhone Pro? Scan the room with any RoomPlan app that exports
  JSON (RoomScan, openPlan3D) and drop the file in the job folder.

For laptop users: a real quote
  1. In quotes/incoming make a folder named after the address.
  2. Drop in your phone photos, a notes.txt (copy the example), and
     measurements.json if you measured.
  3. /quote <folder name>
  4. Check the assumptions, then send the PDF from your own email.
  5. When they say yes:   /mark Q-1001 accepted
     then                 /invoice Q-1001 deposit
  6. Every Monday:        /chase

Rules the pack lives by
  - In this pack, Claude drafts and you send, from your own email or
    phone. (The phone app is different: once you set up SMS and email
    there, it can send quotes and follow-ups for you. See HELP.txt.)
  - Claude only uses prices from your price list.
  - Everything stays in this folder. Back the folder up.

When you want more
  Ask in plain English. Good first extensions people build:
    "Make a page of before-and-after photos I can send clients."
    "Show me my win rate on quotes this year."
    "Plan my week's jobs in a sensible driving order."
  Claude will explain what it needs and build it inside this folder.
