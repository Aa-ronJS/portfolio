# Meta ads: first campaign for the Chasem course (painters)

> **Superseded for the $99 app.** This file sells the $249 laptop pack as "no subscription, ever" and
> must not be used for the Chasem app, which is now for every trade. The current plan is [`PAID-ADS-PLAN.md`](PAID-ADS-PLAN.md).

Positioning: "Own it." A quoting tool that is theirs. No subscription,
ever. They set it up themselves in an afternoon, on their own laptop,
following the videos. The ad sells ownership and the end of per-job fees;
the page offers the hosted version to anyone who would rather not build.

## Structure

One campaign, sales objective, Purchase as the conversion event
(fallback: Lead until Purchase has data). Two ad sets, same creative,
different audiences, AUD 30 to 40 a day each, five days. Australia,
25 to 60. Advantage+ placements on; if the video exists, force Reels and
Stories to use the 9:16 cut. Meta has removed much detailed interest
targeting, so use Advantage+ audience with interests as suggestions and
let the first line of the ad do the targeting.

- **Ad set A, the painter.** Advantage+ audience with suggestions:
  house painting, painting and decorating, Dulux, Haymes Paint,
  Taubmans, Wattyl, Master Painters Australia, tradie, small business
  owner. Use whichever of these exist in your account.
- **Ad set B, the person who does the admin.** Same suggestions,
  narrowed by bookkeeping, Xero, small business admin. 28 to 60. The
  partner or office manager who sits at the laptop at 9pm.

Kill rules after AUD 150 spent per ad set: link click-through under 1%
or cost per landing-page view over AUD 3, pause. Demo use rate under 10%
of visitors, the page is the problem, not the ad. Leads but no
purchases after 300 visitors, test AUD 199 before touching the copy.

## Creative

**Video (preferred):** 30 seconds. Open on the phone taking a photo of
a wall, cut to the laptop typing `/quote`, speed through the run, land
on the PDF total, then a two-second shot of the price list file with
the cursor changing a number: "your prices". End card: "Own
it. $249 once." and the URL. Captions on; most Reels play muted.

**Still (launch tonight if the video is not ready):** screenshot the
landing page hero's quote card on a plain background, headline "Own

Both are in `launch/creative/`.

## Landing

Send every ad to the page root with UTM tags:
`?utm_source=meta&utm_medium=paid&utm_campaign=painters-launch&utm_content=A-evening`
The demo is the conversion mechanism on the page. The QR is for print
and for people watching the ad on a desktop.

## What not to say

No income or savings figures you cannot show. No "Anthropic approved"
or logos. No "replaces ServiceM8" in ad copy; "no subscription, ever"
makes the point without naming anyone. Do not target under 25. Do not
say "AI builds it for you"; say "you set it up yourself", which is
true and is the point. "Own it" must stay literally true: it
is files on their laptop, and the page says so.
