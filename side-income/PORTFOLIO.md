# Small-income portfolio: many machines, a few dollars each

Rule: every machine must sit somewhere buyers already turn up (a
marketplace, an app store, a voice library). Nothing here needs its own
marketing, because marketing isn't passive.

Claude builds and maintains everything. Aaron's part is a one-time "admin
afternoon": creating the accounts, doing the tax interviews (W-8BEN for
the US platforms) and clicking publish.

The weekly figures are guesses for after it ramps up (2–3 months), not
promises. Most of these platforms publish no average earnings. Expect a
few machines to earn nothing and one or two to carry the rest. That
spread is the reason to have many.

Midpoints add up to ≈$55 a week. That works only if the spread holds and
some machines beat their guess.

## Left out on purpose
- **Faceless YouTube/TikTok:** demonetised as "inauthentic" content since mid-2025.
- **Our own SEO sites:** AI answers in search took the traffic.
- **Selling data and microtasks to AI-training apps (FirstHand, Neon, ...):**
  it's work by the minute, not passive, and some licences are broad
  (cloning of the voice and face).
- **Pay-to-overwrite homepages:** fun, but there are already a dozen copies.

Build first what needs nothing from Aaron except an account: 1 → 6 → 4
→ 2. Do 3 and 5 on the admin afternoon, because they need him in
person. Put up 7 and 8 last, and only if 1 shows people like Gerald.
