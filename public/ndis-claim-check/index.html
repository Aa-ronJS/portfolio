    Receipts

    Where every number on this page comes from.

    Nothing here is a statistic I found in somebody's marketing. The only figures this page produces are the ones your own file produces. The rules it measures you against are these, and they are all somebody else's documents.

    • NDIS, Securing the NDIS for future generations The reform package containing the change to the claiming window. The Amendment Bill passed Parliament on 19 August 2026. Check this page for its current status before you rely on the date.
    • Department of Health, Disability and Ageing, About the changes to the NDIS The Commonwealth explanation of what changes and when, including the reduction of the claiming period from two years to 90 days from 1 December 2026.
    • NDIS, Changes to payments and claims from 1 July 2026 The changes already in force this financial year, which is the ground the December change lands on.
    • NDIS, Claims and payments troubleshooting The official list of what goes wrong with a payment request and what the portal says when it does.
    • NDIS, Bulk Payment Request template The sixteen column CSV the portal accepts. It is the format the tool above recognises without being told anything, and the one the upload checks are written against.

    Reasonable questions

    Does my participant data leave my computer?

    No. The file is read by your browser with the file reader built into it, and the arithmetic happens on your own machine. There is no server behind this page to send anything to. You do not have to take that on faith: open your browser's network tab and drop the file in, or disconnect from the internet first. The tool will still work, which is the proof. Participant numbers are masked on screen even locally, because there is no reason for them to be readable over your shoulder.

    My export does not look like the portal's file.

    That is expected, every system exports something different. The page reads the header row and asks you which column is which. As long as there is a date the support was delivered and an amount, it can do the main calculation, and every other column you can point it at adds another check.

    Is 1 December 2026 certain?

    The Amendment Bill passed Parliament on 19 August 2026 and 1 December 2026 is the date published for the claiming change. Commencement dates can move, so the two government links above are on this page rather than being summarised by me, and you should click them. What is not in question either way is the direction: the window is being shortened, and knowing your own claim lag is useful whatever date it starts.
