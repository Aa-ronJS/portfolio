The layer stack, bottom to top

This is the whole recipe. Nine layers, one prototype link, and the same four glass numbers on every translucent surface. Rebuild it in Figma in this order.

  1. 01
    PlateThe AI image. 1440 × 900 frame, image fill set to Crop, subject on the right third, sky and ground left empty for type. Prompt for a cinematic 3D render or photograph with one light source and negative space. No filters on it.
  2. 02
    ScrimA rectangle with two linear gradient fills: black 55% → 0% from the top, and black 62% → 0% from the bottom. This is what lets white type sit on a bright plate.
  3. 03
    HeadlineDisplay face at roughly 210px, weight 600, tracking -3.5%, line height 86%. Two words, second one in the accent colour. Placed low-left so the subject overlaps its last letters.
  4. 04
    CutoutDuplicate the plate, run a background-removal plugin on the copy, and leave it exactly in place above the headline. The subject now stands in front of the type. This one layer is what makes the whole thing look expensive.
  5. 05
    NavAuto layout, space between, 62px tall, radius 999. Glass spec: fill white 7%, stroke white 14% at 1px, background blur 18, inner shadow white 10% at y=1. Logo left, four links centre, an icon and a white pill right.
  6. 06
    CopyEyebrow in mono, 11px, tracking 18%, with a 34px rule before it. Subhead at 18px, 145% line height, white at 86%. Two pills: one solid accent, one glass.
  7. 07
    Stat chipsThree glass pills, right aligned, 8px dot in the accent with a glow. The number in the display face at 18px, the label in body at 12px. Real figures only.
  8. 08
    Pager"01" in display at 32px, a 130px hairline at white 25% with a 20% accent segment, "05" in mono. Bottom right.
  9. 09
    Card, two statesOne component with a collapsed and an expanded variant, same layer names inside both. Prototype: On click → change to expanded, Smart Animate, ease out, 620ms. Entrance: each layer above gets an After delay from 50ms to 900ms in 100ms steps, moving up 24px into place.

What you need

Nothing here needs a paid plan except the video demo, and even that has a free route.

  • Canvas
    Figma, free plan. Frames, auto layout, variants, Smart Animate and background blur are all on the free tier.
  • Plate
    An image model. Midjourney, Flux or GPT Image for the scene. Ideogram or GPT Image if you want the headline rendered as 3D letters inside the picture. Ask for 16:10, one light source, negative space.
  • Cutout
    Background removal. Figma's built-in Remove Background, the remove.bg plugin, or rembg on your own machine. The cutout on this page came from rembg.
  • Type
    One display face, one body face, one mono. The pack uses Google fonts. This page uses the portfolio's Clash Display, Satoshi and Plex Mono.
  • Icons
    A thin line set. Lucide or Iconify, 1.8px stroke, round caps. Never more than three on a hero.
  • Glass
    Four numbers. Fill 7%, stroke 14%, blur 18, inner highlight 10%. Write them as styles once and reuse them on every surface.
  • Motion
    Smart Animate. Two variants of the card and staggered After-delay entrances. Ease out, 400 to 700ms. Nothing else.
  • Mockup
    A device scene. Generate a laptop in a matching environment with a blank screen, then map the frame onto it with a perspective mockup plugin such as Mockup or Artboard Studio.
  • Demo
    Screen recording, 4:5. Record the prototype in Present mode, cut it after the mockup shot in CapCut or Premiere, export WebM and MP4 at 1080 × 1350.
  • Time
    An afternoon. Half of it goes on getting the plate right. The UI layer is forty minutes once the styles exist.
