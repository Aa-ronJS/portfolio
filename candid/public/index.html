How a period runs

01

Write the brief

Seven questions. They become the review form, the eligibility check and the scoring rubric. The brief freezes when the period opens.

02

Send the links

Fund 3 to 50 seats, get one personal link per seat, send each to someone you recruited. They accept on their phone and write as they use your product.

03

Read, then unlock

Reviews arrive together when the open stage ends. Issues are listed as teasers and priced by peer score. Unlock the ones worth fixing.

Why it stays honest

Every review carries a real name, state and photo. For B2B, their business too. Issues are reported anonymously.
Reviewers are anonymous to each other: every review is rated blind by three peers from the same period.
Flags are triaged by AI and decided by a person. Nothing is binned by a machine.
Reviewers are paid for the submission, never for whether you adopt it.
You pay before you read. Nobody can take an idea and not pay for it.
Reviewers declare whether they knew you. Too many friends and you are told.
Quote a review publicly only with the reviewer's consent. The paid-review disclosure travels with it.
