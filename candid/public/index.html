How it works

01

Write the brief

Seven questions. The answers set the review form, who qualifies, and how reviews are scored. The brief is locked once the period opens.

02

Send the links

Fund 3 to 50 seats and get one link per seat. Send each link to one person. They accept on their phone and write the review while they use the product.

03

Read and unlock

Reviews are released together when the open stage ends. Issues show as one-line summaries with a score and a price. Unlock the ones you want.

What keeps it honest

Reviews are signed with the reviewer's name, state and photo, and their business for B2B. Issues are anonymous.
Three other reviewers rate each review without seeing who wrote it.
Flagged reviews are checked by AI and decided by a person.
Reviewers are paid for submitting, not for whether you act on it.
Issue text is shown after you pay for it.
Reviewers say whether they knew you. If most did, you are warned.
A review can be quoted publicly only if the reviewer agrees. The quote includes a note that they took part in a paid study.
