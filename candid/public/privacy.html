Privacy

Last updated 12 October 2026.

What is stored

  • Businesses: the account email, the business name, the brief, credits and the ledger, reviews and issues received, and an activity log.
  • Reviewers: name, state, photo, business name and role if given, email, PayID, the reviews and issues you submit, your peer ratings, and your reputation score.
  • Payments: Stripe processes cards. Candid stores the Stripe session id and the amount, not card details.

Who sees what

  • The business sees your name, state, photo and review. It does not see your email or PayID, and it does not see who reported an issue.
  • Other reviewers see review text only, with no name.
  • Candid's operators see everything, to decide flags and make payouts.
  • Review and issue text is sent to Anthropic's Claude to score, categorise and triage it. It is not used to train models.
  • Email is sent through Resend. Nothing is sold or shared for advertising.

Keeping and deleting

  • A business keeps read access to its periods. A reviewer can ask for their account to be closed; entries are anonymised, not deleted, because the business paid for them.
  • Data is hosted on Vercel and a Postgres provider. Ask at the address on the landing page for a copy of what is held about you.
