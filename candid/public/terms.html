Terms

Last updated 12 October 2026. These terms cover businesses that run review periods on Candid and reviewers who take part.

What Candid is

Candid runs private review periods. A business writes a brief, prepays credits and invites reviewers. Reviewers use the product, write a review and rate three other reviews. Candid holds the credits, applies the rules below and pays reviewers.

Businesses

  • Credits are prepaid and non-refundable, except that unused seats are refunded to the credit balance when a period closes. Credits expire 12 months after purchase.
  • A seat costs $24: $20 to the reviewer and 20% to Candid. Issue unlocks cost $10 to $30 and 80% goes to the reporter.
  • Reviews are private to the business. A review may be quoted publicly only if the reviewer agreed when submitting it, and the quote must include the note that the reviewer took part in a paid product study.
  • A business does not decide flags on its own reviewers. Candid does.
  • A business must deliver the free product or job described in its brief to each reviewer who accepts a seat.

Reviewers

  • You are paid $20 for a written review and three peer ratings, and a share of any issue the business unlocks. Payment is by PayID after the period settles.
  • Reviews must be your own, truthful account of using the product. Boilerplate, copied text, reviews of a product you did not use, and abuse are breaches. A breach forfeits the fee and may suspend or ban your account.
  • A flag on your review is checked by AI and decided by a person within 7 days. You may reply once. If no decision is made in 7 days, your fee is released.
  • Your name, state and photo (and business, for B2B) are shown to the business with your review. Issues you report are anonymous to the business. Other reviewers do not see who you are.
  • You are an independent participant, not an employee or contractor of Candid or the business. Payments may be taxable income; that is your responsibility.

Accounts and changes

  • An email address is an account. Keep your sign-in codes to yourself.
  • Candid may change these terms. Changes apply to periods started after the change.
  • Candid is operated from Australia and these terms are governed by the law of South Australia.
