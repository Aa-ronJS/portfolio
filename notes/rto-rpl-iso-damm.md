## RTO track

### How the money works
**1. Writing assessment tools: paid per unit, no TAE needed**
- About **$300–1,000 per unit**, agreed per unit, sometimes with a paid trial unit first. Invoiced on an ABN, usually 14–30 day terms.
- Higher margin: license the same tool to several RTOs (non-exclusive). Ready-made kits sell for $230–650 per unit.

**2. Assessing students: paid per candidate or per unit, TAE needed**

| Model | Rate (work plan) |
|---|---|
| Per unit marked | ~$30–80 |
| Per completed assessment | ~$50 (one listing) |
| Per RPL candidate (whole diploma) | ~$250–700 |
| Hourly plus per-unit rate | ~$45–46/hr plus per unit |
| Specialist RPL, hourly | ~$100–150/hr |
| Validation | ~$100–300 per unit |

Most RTOs take assessors on as ABN contractors and pay against a monthly invoice of completed assessments. Per-candidate RPL for experienced professionals pays best per hour.

### The rule that decides what you can assess
1. A TAE credential the RTO accepts (TAE40122 or whatever the RTO currently accepts; the rules changed in 2025).
2. The qualification being assessed, at or above that level.
3. Current industry experience in that field.

### Income target during the CPL year
Austudy is reduced by income over its free area (2026: $539/fn free area, then 50c per dollar to $646, then 60c). Deemed income on savings uses most of that free area, so earnings above about $150/wk start reducing Austudy, which reaches zero at about $880/wk. Aim for **under ~$150/wk or well over ~$1,000/wk**; the band in between gains least. Check with Services Australia how ABN income is assessed and whether the Student Income Bank applies to it.

## ISO track (parked to 2029, with a cheap 2027 option)

| Item | Detail |
|---|---|
| Course | Lead auditor course, 3–5 days, CQI/IRCA or Exemplar certified. About US$900–1,500 online or AU$2–3.5k in person (est.). Real cost if taken in weekdays: plus lost OD days, so roughly $7–10k. |
| IRCA grades | Associate (course passed) → Auditor (at least 4 audits, 20 audit days, some on site) → Lead Auditor (plus 3 audits and 15 days as lead). Check current rules. |
| Certification body | A witnessed audit is mandatory (ISO/IEC 17021-1 cl. 7.2.4). For 27001: trainee on one initial or recertification audit plus one surveillance audit. |
| Certification bodies to ask about trainee intake | Bureau Veritas, BSI, SAI, LRQA, DEKRA. Ask which standard they're short of auditors in. |
| Contract audit day rate | ~$700–1,200 (est.), below the current contract rate |
| Longer-term links | 27001 lead auditor counts as IRAP Category B; NDIS, HVA and greenhouse auditing build on the 9001 course |

## DAMM (TetraFlex) links

**What DAMM's own training looks like**
- DAMM delivers it for partners and end users: 1–5 day courses, at the customer's site or at DAMM's headquarters in Denmark.
- The main end-user course is a 4-day workshop. Graduates should be able to operate and maintain the system and train internal maintenance staff.
- Partners get materials, webinars and videos on DAMM's partner extranet.
- No sign of local Australian delivery was found.

**Plan**
1. Ask for a place on DAMM's 4-day end-user workshop as part of the current rollout.
2. With that course and the TAE, take on TetraFlex training (administrators, dispatchers, maintainers, users) as part of the contract, at each migration phase.
3. Later, and only if it makes sense: offer to be a local training partner for DAMM or its Australian partner.

**Constraints**
- Material written under the current contract belongs to the client; a private training business would have to use DAMM's partner material or your own content.
- Groups in this niche are small (a site has about 5–15 comms staff), so pricing would be per group, roughly $15–30k for a 2–4 day course (est.).

**Open questions**
1. Will the project fund your place on the DAMM workshop?
2. Who delivers TetraFlex training in Australia now?
3. How many Australian sites run DAMM?

**Where radio feeds the RTO work:** realistic critical-infrastructure scenarios (like the Northgate mine radio upgrade in sample 1) are what set your tools apart from generic AI-written ones.
