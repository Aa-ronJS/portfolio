# How sure are we?

Nobody can be 100% sure an idea is a winner from a desk. Three things
decide it, and only the first can be settled by research:

1. **Is the way clear?** No identical product, no blocking patent, a
   real problem, a company with a reason to buy. Research can settle
   this.
2. **Does it work?** Only a bench test can settle this.
3. **Will a company pay?** Only pitching can settle this.

This file tracks every open doubt on the top ideas and what would close
it. "Cleared" means cleared by web research, which is weaker than an
attorney's search.

## Where that leaves us

- **Closest to sure on the desk questions:** the reproof tab. Every
  doubt that research can close is closed. What remains needs a jacket,

  bench test: about A$100 to A$150 and a few weeks of wear.
