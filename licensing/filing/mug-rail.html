Draining accessory for a dishwasher rack

Provisional patent specification
Applicant and inventor: Aaron Steele

Field

This invention relates to accessories for dishwasher racks.

Background

Drinking vessels are placed inverted in a dishwasher rack. Many have a recessed base, in which wash water collects and remains after the cycle. Manufacturers advise leaning such vessels at the sides of the rack, and users tilt the whole rack, with limited effect.

Summary of the invention

In one aspect the invention provides: a retrofit accessory for a dishwasher rack having upstanding tines, comprising an elongate body with engagement formations on its underside for releasable attachment to a row of tines, and a plurality of spaced raised supports on its upper side, each positioned to raise one side of the rim of an inverted drinking vessel so that the vessel rests at an incline and liquid drains from its base.

Brief description of the drawing

Figure 1 shows two inverted vessels on a rack in side view: on the left resting level with liquid retained on the base; on the right resting on a support of the accessory, inclined, with liquid draining.

Detailed description

The body is a strip of dishwasher-safe polymer with a resilient covering. Slots in its underside receive the tines of the rack and hold the body in place.

Each support is a wedge approximately 12 millimetres high. An inverted vessel of 80 millimetres diameter resting with one side of its rim on a support is inclined at approximately 8 to 9 degrees.

A lip on each support resists the vessel sliding under the force of the spray.

Variations

The following variations are within the scope of the invention, alone or in combination:

  1. Slots sized to fit a range of tine spacings and wire diameters.
  2. Individual clip-on supports in place of an elongate body.
  3. Support height selected to give an incline of 6 to 15 degrees.

The embodiments described are examples only. Features of one embodiment may be combined with features of another.
