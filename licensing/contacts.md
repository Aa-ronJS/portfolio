## Lifetime Brands form: what it asks

Have these ready for the pan click and mug rail:

1. Product name
2. The problem you're solving
3. Why you're solving it
4. Market research done
5. The main feature that sets it apart
6. Utility patent search done? (yes or no)
7. Patent protection filed? (yes or no, with date)
8. CAD drawings or renderings? (yes or no)
9. Video links
10. Images or sell sheets (upload)
11. Hand-made or professionally built?
12. Prototype built? Ever manufactured? Trademark filed?
13. Submitted to other companies? When?

The answers to 2 to 5 are in each product file. The sell sheet PDFs are
in `licensing/sell-sheets/`.
