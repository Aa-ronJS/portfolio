# Voice

All prose written in this repo that speaks as Aaron (application answers, cover
notes, emails, LinkedIn, site copy unless the design brief says otherwise) is
written in his register, not a designed one and not an AI's. It was studied from
a long sample of his own writing. The rules:

- Contractions everywhere they'd occur naturally: I'd, wasn't, that's, doesn't.
- Sentences carry a thought all the way through, connected with "because",
  "which meant", "so", "and". Don't chop them into punchy fragments.
- Understatement over punch. "Which settled the question of whether the risk
  was real", not "Nobody had to argue about whether it was real."
- Honest hedging is part of the voice, not a weakness to edit out: "roughly",
  "about", "maybe 20-30%", "probably", "I think". Never round a hedged number
  into a confident one.
- Dry asides are allowed mid-sentence, without a drum roll before them.
- Plain professional Australian English. Not Crocodile Dundee, no "mate", no
  "heaps". Just how a mid-30s Adelaide IT consultant actually writes.
- Things happened in an order; tell them in that order. Story before moral, if
  the moral is needed at all.

Banned, because they're AI tells or hyperbole he wouldn't use:

- The antithesis pattern repeated as a rhythm: "That is X, not Y." Once in a
  long document is fine; as a cadence it's a tell.
- Bolded lead-ins, rule-of-three flourishes, "Here's the thing", rhetorical
  questions answered immediately.
- Em-dash-heavy staccato and one-word sentences for effect.
- Hype words: game-changing, transformative, leverage (as a verb), passionate,
  supercharge, seamless, robust, delve.
- Claims that outrun the evidence. Every figure must trace to something on the
  portfolio page or in the repos.

The portfolio page itself (`public/index.html`) has its own designed voice
(clipped, no contractions) recorded in `PREFLIGHT.md`. That's deliberate and
stays. This file governs everything written as Aaron speaking.
